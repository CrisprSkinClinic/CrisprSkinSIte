There is no single cream that fixes acanthosis nigricans. Treatment works best in layers.

## 1. Treat the Cause

Improving insulin sensitivity is the most effective step: gradual weight loss, activity and food choices, plus medicines where your doctor advises. Patches often fade in step with this.

## 2. Creams to Soften and Lighten

Retinoid creams, urea, salicylic acid and lactic acid soften thickened skin. They can irritate, so we start gently, especially on the neck and folds. Skin-lightening creams used without advice can burn and darken skin.

## 3. Procedures for Stubborn Patches

Superficial chemical peels and certain lasers help in selected people once the cause is being treated. Skin tags in the area can be removed. Results take several sessions and need maintenance.

## What to Avoid

Scrubbing, harsh soaps, bleaching creams and home remedies containing lemon or strong acids. They irritate and can leave marks. Our page on [post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation](/conditions/post-inflammatory-hyperpigmentation) explains why.

## Realistic Expectations

Improvement takes months. Patches may return if insulin resistance returns, which is why the cause matters more than the cream. See [acanthosis nigricans](/conditions/acanthosis-nigricans) and our [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels).

## Realistic Timelines

Lifestyle change and treatment of the underlying cause may lighten patches within three to six months, but full normalisation can take a year or more, and some residual darkness may remain. Topical treatments take six to twelve weeks to show effect. Patients who expect quick results from a single cream are often disappointed, and this is why multi-pronged treatment works best.

## Topical Options in Detail

- **Retinoid creams (tretinoin, adapalene):** reduce thickening and darkening over weeks. Start at night, three nights a week, and moisturise.
- **Urea (10 to 40 percent) and lactic acid:** gentle softening of thick skin, well tolerated in folds.
- **Salicylic acid:** removes scale. Use cautiously to avoid irritation.
- **Vitamin D analogues:** sometimes used by dermatologists.
- **Skin-lightening agents:** azelaic acid or other mild lighteners may be used with advice. Strong or unregulated bleaching products can irritate and darken skin, and should be avoided.

## Procedures

**Chemical peels:** superficial peels, repeated at intervals, can lighten the skin when combined with home treatment. See [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels).

**Laser:** certain lasers can reduce thickness and darkness in selected patients, and laser hair reduction helps when hair in the folds contributes to friction and ingrown hairs.

**Treatment of skin tags:** small tags in the neck and folds can be removed. See [skin tags](/conditions/skin-tags).

## Medicines That Treat the Root Cause

When a physician diagnoses insulin resistance, prediabetes or PCOS, medicines such as metformin may improve both metabolism and skin. These are prescribed and monitored by your doctor, not sold as skin treatments.

## Everyday Care

Wear soft, loose collars. Wash gently with a soap-free cleanser, and avoid rubbing with a towel. Keep skin folds clean and dry. Sunscreen protects against further darkening, as sun can worsen pigmentation.

## Setting Expectations

You may notice that patches lighten as weight and insulin levels improve, even without much topical treatment. Others have more stubborn disease, requiring peels or lasers. We tailor the plan, review at intervals, and focus on health first.
