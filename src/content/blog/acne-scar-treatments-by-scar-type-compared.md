No single treatment suits every acne scar. The best results come from naming the scar type, then choosing and combining treatments to match. Most patients have more than one type.

## The Main Types

- **Icepick scars:** narrow, deep, pit-like holes, usually on the cheeks and temples.
- **Boxcar scars:** wider, round or oval depressions with sharp edges.
- **Rolling scars:** broad, shallow, wavy dips where bands under the skin tether it down.
- **Raised (hypertrophic) scars:** firm bumps, mostly on the chest, back and jawline. See [scar revision](/services/scar-revision).
- **Marks:** flat red or brown patches left after spots. These are not true scars. See [red and brown marks after acne](/blog/red-and-brown-marks-after-acne-vs-true-scars).

## How the Treatments Compare

**Subcision** frees the bands that tether rolling and some boxcar scars, so the skin rises. It is done with a fine needle under local anaesthetic, with bruising for several days. It is often the first step for rolling scars.

**TCA CROSS** applies a strong acid into individual icepick scars to prompt rebuilding from the base. It is done in small sessions every few weeks, and works well for icepick scars with limited downtime.

**Microneedling** creates controlled tiny channels to stimulate collagen. It suits shallow boxcar and rolling scars and overall texture, over a series of sessions. See [microneedling for acne scars](/blog/microneedling-for-acne-scars-what-to-expect).

**Fractional laser** (ablative and non-ablative) removes columns of skin to prompt renewal, for shallow to moderate boxcar scars and texture. Downtime is longer with ablative lasers, and darker skin needs caution. See [fractional laser for acne scars](/blog/fractional-laser-for-acne-scars-recovery-and-results).

**Chemical peels** treat mild texture, marks and tone, but do little for deep scars. See [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels).

**Dermal fillers** can lift some depressed scars for several months, but are temporary.

**Punch excision or elevation** removes or lifts deep boxcar scars surgically, sometimes followed by resurfacing.

## Combining Treatments

A common plan: subcision for rolling scars, TCA CROSS for icepick scars, then microneedling or laser to smooth the surface, with peels for tone. Sessions are spaced to allow healing, and the full programme often takes six to twelve months.

## What Results to Expect

Most people reach a thirty to seventy percent improvement, depending on the type and depth of scars, and on skin type. Deep icepick scars improve least. Complete removal is not realistic.

## Before Starting

- Active acne must be controlled first, or new scars will form. See [acne treatment](/services/acne).
- Tell us if you took isotretinoin recently, as some procedures wait for a few months afterwards.
- Sun protection is essential before and after, especially on Indian skin, which marks easily.
