Many people with acne use too many products, which irritates the skin and makes acne worse. A short, steady routine works better.

## Morning

1. **Cleanse** with a gentle, non-foaming or mildly foaming cleanser.
2. **Treat:** a thin layer of benzoyl peroxide, azelaic acid or niacinamide as advised.
3. **Moisturise** with a light, non-comedogenic gel or lotion, even if skin is oily.
4. **Protect:** a broad-spectrum sunscreen, SPF 30 or more, labelled non-comedogenic or gel-based.

## Night

1. **Cleanse** to remove sunscreen and makeup.
2. **Retinoid** (adapalene or tretinoin) on dry skin, a pea-sized amount for the whole face.
3. **Moisturise.**

Start the retinoid two or three nights a week and build up, since dryness and peeling are common in the first weeks.

## Ingredients That Help

- **Retinoids:** unblock pores and are the foundation.
- **Benzoyl peroxide:** reduces bacteria and inflammation.
- **Azelaic acid:** calms acne and fades marks.
- **Salicylic acid:** useful for blackheads and oily skin.
- **Niacinamide:** helps oil and marks. See [niacinamide for oily skin](/blog/niacinamide-for-oily-and-acne-prone-skin).

## Common Mistakes

- Scrubbing and over-cleansing, which damage the barrier.
- Using several actives at once. See [skincare routine: order and clashes](/blog/skincare-routine-order-and-active-clashes).
- Skipping moisturiser and sunscreen.
- Picking spots, which causes marks and scars.
- Changing products every week.
- Heavy hair oils and makeup that block pores.

## How Long to Wait

Give each change six to eight weeks. See [why acne treatment takes time](/blog/why-acne-treatment-takes-time).
