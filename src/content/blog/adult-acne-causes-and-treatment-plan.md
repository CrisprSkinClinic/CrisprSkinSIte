Acne is not only a teenage problem. Many adults, especially women, have acne in their twenties, thirties and beyond. Adult acne is more stubborn, more inflamed and more likely to leave marks, and it needs a plan, not a shelf of products.

## Why It Happens in Adults

- **Hormones:** cycle changes, PCOS, stopping contraception, stress hormones. See [hormonal acne](/conditions/hormonal-acne).
- **Stress and poor sleep,** which raise oil production and inflammation.
- **Products:** heavy creams, makeup, hair oils and sunscreens that block pores.
- **Medicines:** steroids, some hormones, lithium and some anti-epileptics.
- **Diet:** high-sugar foods and, in some people, dairy and whey protein. See [food and acne](/blog/food-and-acne-what-the-evidence-says).
- **Picking and over-scrubbing,** which inflame the skin and cause marks.

## How It Looks

Deep, tender bumps on the lower face and jawline, a few whiteheads and blackheads, and slow-healing marks. Skin is often also dry or sensitive from products used to fight acne.

## Treatment Plan

1. **A retinoid at night,** the base of most plans, started slowly three nights a week.
2. **Benzoyl peroxide or azelaic acid** by day, to calm inflammation.
3. **Short courses of antibiotic tablets** for inflamed acne, never for long periods alone.
4. **Hormone-directed treatment** such as certain pills or spironolactone in suitable women. See [hormonal acne medicines explained](/blog/hormonal-acne-medicines-explained).
5. **Isotretinoin** for severe, scarring or persistent acne. See [isotretinoin: what to know](/blog/isotretinoin-for-acne-what-to-know-before-during-and-after).
6. **Gentle skin care:** a mild cleanser, a non-comedogenic moisturiser and a sunscreen.

## What to Expect

Two to three months for clear improvement, and six months for the best results. Maintenance with a retinoid prevents relapse.

## Preventing Marks and Scars

Do not pick, treat early, and use sunscreen daily. See [red and brown marks versus scars](/blog/red-and-brown-marks-after-acne-vs-true-scars) and our [acne scar treatment](/services/acne-scars).

## When to Test for PCOS

Irregular periods, excess hair, scalp thinning or a dark neck call for a check. See [when to test for PCOS](/blog/hormonal-acne-and-pcos-when-to-test).
