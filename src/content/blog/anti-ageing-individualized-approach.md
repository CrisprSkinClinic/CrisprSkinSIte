When someone says they want to "look less tired" or "do something about ageing," the honest first step is figuring out which specific changes are actually bothering them -- because ageing skin involves several distinct, simultaneous processes, and no single product addresses all of them.

## The Different Components of Skin Ageing

[Volume loss](/blog/fillers-vs-botox), changes in texture and [pigmentation](/services/pigmentation), deepening [expression lines](/blog/botox-natural-results), and gradual loss of skin firmness are all part of "ageing," but they're distinct processes with distinct treatments. Two people who both say they want to look more refreshed might need completely different approaches depending on which of these is most prominent for them.

## Why We Ask What Actually Bothers You

Not every visible change needs treatment, and some patients want to address one specific thing rather than everything at once. We spend real time understanding what's actually motivating the visit -- volume loss, texture, expression lines -- since that shapes the entire plan far more than a generic "anti-ageing package" would.

## Skincare as the Foundation

A well-structured skincare routine using evidence-backed [active ingredients](/blog/retinol-vs-retinoids-how-to-start) tends to be the foundation most other treatment builds on, and it's often more consequential long-term than any single in-clinic procedure. We prioritize getting this right before or alongside anything else.

## Sun Protection Isn't Optional Here

[UV exposure](/conditions/photoageing) is the single most significant modifiable driver of premature skin ageing. Any anti-ageing plan that doesn't treat sun protection as a genuinely core component is missing the highest-leverage piece of the puzzle.

## Setting Honest Expectations

Anti-ageing treatment manages and slows visible signs of ageing -- it doesn't stop or reverse the process itself. We build plans around meaningful, realistic improvement in what actually matters to you, rather than promising a complete transformation no treatment can deliver.

## What a First Visit Looks Like

We begin by asking what you notice when you look in the mirror and what you hope to change. We examine your skin in good light and may photograph it for comparison. We also ask about sun exposure, smoking, medicines, past treatments and any tendency to scar or pigment. From this we draw up a short plan with priorities, rather than offering everything at once.

## Treatments Fall Into a Few Groups

Some treatments improve skin quality, such as peels, resurfacing and carefully chosen medical-grade skincare. Others relax the muscles that deepen lines, and others restore lost volume. Each group has its place, and the order matters, since for example improving skin texture first can make the benefit of later treatments clearer. Our pages on [skin rejuvenation](/services/skin-rejuvenation) and [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels) describe some of these options.

## Starting Early Versus Starting Late

There is no single "right age" to begin. Sun protection and a sensible routine help at any age, and most in-clinic treatments are more useful when matched to visible concerns than to a birthday. Fewer, well-chosen treatments often look better than many unnecessary ones.

## Reviewing Your Plan

Skin changes with time, so we review how you are doing and adjust. Some people drop treatments they no longer need, and others add new ones as their concerns change.
