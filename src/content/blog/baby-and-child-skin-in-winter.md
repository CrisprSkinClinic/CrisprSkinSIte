Children's skin has a thinner barrier than adults', so it loses moisture quickly and reacts faster in dry weather.

## Bathing

Short, lukewarm baths, once a day or less if the skin is dry. Use a mild, fragrance-free wash only where needed, and pat dry gently.

## Moisturise After Every Bath

Apply a plain, thick moisturiser within three minutes of the bath, and again later in the day on dry areas. Ointments and creams work better than thin lotions.

## Dress Comfortably

Layers of soft cotton are better than a single thick woollen layer next to the skin. Wool and synthetic fibres can irritate. Do not overdress babies, as sweating also irritates.

## Lips and Cheeks

Dry cheeks and lips are common. A plain petrolatum-based balm helps. Avoid licking the lips, which makes them worse.

## Watch for Eczema

Itchy, red, dry patches in the creases of elbows and knees, on cheeks or behind the ears may be eczema. See our guide to [managing childhood eczema](/blog/managing-childhood-eczema) and [common childhood skin problems](/blog/common-childhood-skin-conditions).
