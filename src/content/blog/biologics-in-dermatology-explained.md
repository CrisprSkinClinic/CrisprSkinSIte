Biologics are among the most talked-about advances in dermatology, and also among the most misunderstood. Here is a plain account.

## What They Are

Biologics are medicines made from living cells that block one specific signal driving inflammation. Because they are targeted, they work differently from older medicines that dampen the immune system more broadly.

## Where They Are Used

They are used for severe or difficult-to-control [psoriasis](/conditions/moderate-to-severe-psoriasis), some forms of severe [atopic dermatitis](/conditions/moderate-to-severe-atopic-dermatitis), and certain other conditions such as [chronic hives](/conditions/antihistamine-resistant-urticaria) and [hidradenitis suppurativa](/conditions/hidradenitis-suppurativa), depending on the specific medicine. Not every biologic is used for every condition.

## Who Is Considered

People with extensive disease, disease at sensitive sites, joint involvement, or poor response to simpler treatments. Many patients never need one, and we do not reach for them first (see the [psoriasis treatment ladder](/blog/psoriasis-treatment-ladder)).

## What Happens Before Starting

A full review, blood tests, and screening for infections such as tuberculosis and hepatitis B. Monitoring continues afterwards. [JAK inhibitors](/blog/jak-inhibitors-safety-and-monitoring), which are not biologics, have their own monitoring needs.

## A Measured View

They are powerful tools used within a longer treatment plan. The aim is a plan that works for you and that you can sustain, with the least treatment needed to keep you well. You can read more about [biologics and advanced therapy](/services/biologics) at our clinic.

## How Biologics Are Given

Most biologics are given as injections under the skin, which many patients learn to do at home after training, or as infusions in a clinic. Doses are spaced over weeks or months, depending on the medicine. You will be shown how to store and handle the medicine and what to do if a dose is missed.

## Safety and Everyday Life

Because biologics affect immune signalling, infections need prompt attention. Tell us if you have fever, a persistent cough or a new infection, and let any doctor treating you know that you are on a biologic. Live vaccines are generally avoided while on treatment, and other vaccines are best reviewed with us in advance. Pregnancy plans should be discussed before starting.

## What Results to Expect

Some people notice improvement within weeks, though full benefit may take a few months. Not everyone responds to the first biologic, and if one does not work well enough, another may be tried. Some people can later reduce or space out treatment, but this is decided individually.

## Is It Worth It?

For the right person, a biologic can transform quality of life, and the effort of screening and follow-up is worthwhile. For others, simpler treatments are enough. If you are unsure which group you belong to, a consultation will help.
