Good skin care is built over decades. What helps most changes with age, and so does what is worth paying for.

## In Your Twenties: Protect

- **Daily broad-spectrum sunscreen,** the single best anti-ageing step.
- **Gentle cleansing and a simple moisturiser.**
- **Treat acne properly** to prevent marks and scars. See [acne treatment](/services/acne).
- **Start a retinoid** if acne or early texture changes appear.
- **Avoid tanning and smoking.**

## In Your Thirties: Prevent and Even Out

- Continue sunscreen, and add a **night retinoid.** See [retinol and retinoids: how to start](/blog/retinol-vs-retinoids-how-to-start).
- An **antioxidant serum** in the morning if you like.
- **Gentle peels** for tone, marks and texture. See [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels).
- **Treat pigmentation** early. See [pigmentation](/services/pigmentation).
- **Light Botox** for the earliest expression lines, if desired.

## In Your Forties: Restore

- Everything above, with the retinoid as the base.
- **Microneedling or fractional laser** for texture, pores and early laxity. See [microneedling](/blog/microneedling-for-acne-scars-what-to-expect).
- **Botox** for expression lines and **fillers** for volume loss in cheeks and chin. See [Botox](/services/botox) and [fillers](/services/fillers).
- **Check for skin cancers** and sun damage with a yearly skin review.

## For All Ages

- A balanced diet, good sleep, exercise and no smoking help the skin as much as products. See [diet and skin](/blog/diet-and-skin-what-evidence-actually-shows).
- Do not spend on supplements and "boosters" without evidence. See [supplements for skin and hair](/blog/supplements-for-skin-and-hair-what-helps-and-what-does-not).

## What to Skip

Strong home peels and scrubs, unproven injectable "boosters", very cheap clinics and any treatment that promises to make you look years younger overnight.

## Pace and Skin Type

On brown skin, go gently and test first, since irritation leaves marks. Add one change at a time.
