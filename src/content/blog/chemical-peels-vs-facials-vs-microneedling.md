Peels, facials and microneedling are often mixed up, but they are different treatments with different depths and aims. Choosing well saves time and money.

## Facials

**What they do.** Cleansing, steaming, extraction, massage and a mask. They are relaxing and give a short-lived glow.
**Depth.** Surface only.
**Downtime.** None.
**Best for.** Hydration, relaxation and routine skin care.
**Limits.** They do not treat scars, melasma or deep pigmentation. Aggressive extractions and harsh products can cause breakouts, irritation and marks, particularly in brown skin.

## Chemical Peels

**What they do.** An acid dissolves the bonds between dead cells and removes the surface layers, prompting renewal.
**Depth.** Superficial to medium, depending on the acid and strength.
**Downtime.** None to several days of peeling.
**Best for.** Acne, blackheads, dullness, mild to moderate marks, uneven tone and fine lines.
**Limits.** Does little for deep scars. Needs care in darker skin. See [peel types](/blog/chemical-peel-types-glycolic-salicylic-tca-explained).

## Microneedling

**What it does.** Tiny needles create controlled channels that stimulate collagen.
**Depth.** Into the dermis, usually 1 to 2.5 mm.
**Downtime.** One to three days of redness.
**Best for.** Shallow acne scars, texture, large pores and stretch marks. See [microneedling for acne scars](/blog/microneedling-for-acne-scars-what-to-expect).
**Limits.** Not for active acne, infection or keloid-prone skin. Gradual results.

## Comparison at a Glance

| Concern | Facial | Peel | Microneedling |
|---|---|---|---|
| Dull skin, hydration | Yes | Yes | Some |
| Blackheads and acne | Some | Yes | No (not in active acne) |
| Post-acne marks | No | Yes | Some |
| Acne scars (shallow) | No | Little | Yes |
| Deep acne scars | No | No | Partly, with other methods |
| Melasma | No | Gentle peels with care | Not a first choice |
| Fine lines | Little | Yes | Some |

## Combining Them

A dermatologist often combines treatments, in order: control acne, treat marks with peels, then treat scars with microneedling or laser. Doing too much at once irritates the skin and raises the risk of marks.

## What to Ask a Clinic

- Who assesses my skin, and who performs the treatment?
- What is the strength and type of the peel or the depth of needling?
- How many sessions will I need, and what results are realistic?
- How do you protect brown skin from pigment change?
- What is the aftercare?

## Safety Points

All treatments should use sterile single-use needles and consumables. Strict sun protection is needed after peels and microneedling.
