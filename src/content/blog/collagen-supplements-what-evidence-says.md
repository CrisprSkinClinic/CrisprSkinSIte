Collagen is a key structural protein in skin, so it is no surprise that collagen supplements are popular. The marketing, however, runs well ahead of the evidence.

## What the Research Suggests

Some small studies report modest improvements in skin hydration or elasticity with certain collagen supplements. Many of these studies are short, small, or funded by the manufacturers, and results are not consistent.

## The Basic Problem

Collagen taken by mouth is digested into smaller pieces, like other protein. There is no guarantee it is rebuilt as collagen in your skin.

## Is It Harmful

For most healthy people, supplements appear to be reasonably safe, though quality varies between products and some people may react to the source ingredients. Check with your doctor if you have allergies or medical conditions.

## What Protects Collagen Best

Daily sunscreen, not smoking, a balanced diet, and a sound skincare routine are the best-supported ways to preserve collagen. Prescription retinoids also have good evidence.

## A Reasonable Position

If you want to try a supplement, treat it as an optional extra with modest expectations, and do not let it replace the basics.
