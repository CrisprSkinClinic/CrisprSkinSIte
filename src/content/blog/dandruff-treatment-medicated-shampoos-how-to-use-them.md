Most people with dandruff buy a shampoo, use it badly and conclude it does not work. Using it correctly makes a large difference.

## Which Ingredients Work

- **Ketoconazole:** an antifungal that reduces the yeast linked to dandruff.
- **Zinc pyrithione:** antifungal and antibacterial, widely available.
- **Selenium sulphide:** slows skin cell turnover and the yeast.
- **Coal tar:** slows skin renewal, useful for thick scale and psoriasis, with a strong smell.
- **Salicylic acid:** lifts scale, helpful for thick flaking.
- **Ciclopirox:** antifungal and anti-inflammatory.

## How to Use Them

1. **Wet the hair and scalp,** and apply the shampoo to the scalp, not just the hair.
2. **Massage gently** with the fingertips for a minute.
3. **Leave it on for five minutes** (or as the label says), which is the step most people skip.
4. **Rinse well,** then use a conditioner on the lengths.
5. **Use two or three times a week** until flaking is controlled, then once a week to maintain.

## If One Shampoo Stops Working

Rotate between two types, for example ketoconazole and zinc pyrithione, every few weeks, or step up to a prescription product.

## When Flakes Are Not Just Dandruff

Thick silvery plaques may be scalp psoriasis. Red, greasy scale with itch may be seborrhoeic dermatitis, which may need a short steroid lotion. A scaly patch with hair loss in a child may be ringworm. See [scalp psoriasis](/conditions/scalp-psoriasis) and [seborrhoeic dermatitis of the scalp](/conditions/seborrheic-dermatitis-scalp).

## Other Tips

- Wash regularly. Oily hair worsens dandruff.
- Do not scratch or pick.
- Avoid heavy oiling of the scalp.
- Manage stress, since flares follow stress and poor sleep. See [dandruff myths](/blog/dandruff-myths).
- In winter, a gentle moisturising routine helps. See [winter hair fall and dry scalp](/blog/winter-hair-fall-and-dry-scalp).

## When to See a Doctor

See a dermatologist if flaking, redness or itching persists after four weeks of correct shampoo use, if you have hair loss, or if the scalp is sore. See our [dandruff service](/services/dandruff).
