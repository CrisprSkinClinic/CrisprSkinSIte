Fillers work differently in each part of the face, and the best results are subtle. Here is what to expect.

## Cheeks and Mid-Face

Volume loss in the cheeks makes the face look tired and lets the folds deepen. A small amount of filler placed deep over the cheekbone can lift the mid-face and soften the nose-to-mouth lines indirectly. Results last a year or more.

## Lips

Fillers can add shape, definition and a modest amount of volume, and correct asymmetry. Small amounts give natural lips. Overfilling causes the "duck" look. Swelling in the first days exaggerates the final result, so judge after two weeks.

## Under-Eye Hollows (Tear Troughs)

Fillers can soften a hollow under the eye in people whose shadows come from volume loss. They are less effective for dark pigment, puffiness or loose skin, and the area is delicate, with a risk of swelling, a bluish tint and lumps. A careful assessment is essential, and not everyone is a candidate.

## Chin and Jawline

A little filler can balance a recessed chin or define the jawline, and effects last longer in these areas.

## Nose

Non-surgical nose shaping is advanced, with higher risk because of blood vessels. We advise caution and do not offer it routinely.

## Temples and Brow

Selected patients with hollowing benefit, with great care around the vessels.

## What Fillers Cannot Do

They do not tighten loose skin, remove wrinkles caused by movement (Botox does) or treat pigment. Overuse can distort natural proportions.

## Planning a Natural Result

We assess your face as a whole and treat the cause of an area's appearance, often in the cheeks or chin before the lines. Small amounts, placed in stages, give the best result.

## Aftercare

Avoid pressure on the area, alcohol, heat and strenuous exercise for a day or two, and report severe pain, blanching or visual symptoms immediately.
