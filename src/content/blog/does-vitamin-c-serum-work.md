Vitamin C is widely marketed, and it does have a place in a routine. It is not a miracle, and it is worth understanding what it realistically offers.

## What It Does

Vitamin C is an antioxidant. Applied to the skin, it can help protect against some [sun-related damage](/conditions/photoageing), support a brighter-looking tone, and contribute modestly to fading dark marks over time.

## What It Does Not Do

It does not replace [sunscreen](/blog/sunscreen-and-visible-light-indian-skin), and it will not change deep pigmentation or scars overnight. Results build slowly, usually over weeks to months.

## Choosing a Product

Vitamin C is unstable, so packaging and formulation matter. Dark, airtight containers help, and a product that has turned dark brown has likely oxidised. Start with a lower strength if your skin is sensitive.

## Using It

Most people use it in the morning, followed by sunscreen. Introduce it gradually, and stop if it causes persistent stinging or redness.
