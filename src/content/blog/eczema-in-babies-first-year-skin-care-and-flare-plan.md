Eczema often begins in the first months of life, and can be distressing for babies and parents. A steady routine usually keeps it under control.

## What It Looks Like

Dry, red, rough patches on the cheeks, scalp and outer arms and legs. The nappy area is usually spared. Babies rub their face on bedding and scratch more as they grow. Skin can weep or crust when flared.

## Daily Care

1. **Bathe once a day** or less, for five to ten minutes in lukewarm water, with a soap-free cleanser used only where needed.
2. **Moisturise within three minutes** of the bath, with a thick, fragrance-free cream or ointment, and several times through the day.
3. **Dress** in soft cotton and avoid wool next to the skin.
4. **Keep nails short** and use cotton mittens at night if needed.
5. **Avoid** scented products, bubble baths and overheating.

## Treating a Flare

Your doctor will prescribe a mild anti-inflammatory ointment or cream for flare areas, applied thinly once or twice daily for the number of days advised, followed by moisturiser. Do not use adult steroid creams on a baby without advice. See [managing childhood eczema](/blog/managing-childhood-eczema).

## Infections

Yellow crusts, oozing or sores may mean infection and need treatment. Painful clustered blisters or fever with a worsening rash need urgent care, since viral infections of eczema can be serious.

## Food and Allergy

Most babies with eczema do not have a food allergy. Do not stop foods without advice. Early introduction of common allergens such as egg and peanut is usually recommended, and babies with severe eczema should be assessed first. See [food allergy and eczema](/blog/food-allergy-and-eczema-when-to-test-and-when-not-to).

## Vaccines

Eczema is not a reason to delay vaccines. See [vaccinating a child with eczema](/blog/vaccinating-children-with-eczema).

## When to See a Doctor

See a dermatologist if eczema is spreading, weeping, disturbing sleep or not improving with moisturiser. See [baby and child skin in winter](/blog/baby-and-child-skin-in-winter) and our [atopic dermatitis service](/services/atopic-dermatitis).
