Botox and dermal fillers are often mentioned in the same breath, but they work in fundamentally different ways and address different concerns. Knowing the distinction helps you have a more useful conversation at your consultation.

## Botox Relaxes; Fillers Add Volume

[Botox](/services/botox) works by temporarily relaxing the muscles responsible for expression lines -- frown lines, forehead lines, crow's feet. It doesn't add anything to the skin; it reduces the muscle movement that creates certain wrinkles over time.

[Fillers](/services/fillers) work the opposite way -- they add volume directly to areas that have lost it, such as the cheeks or under-eyes, or to enhance contour, as with lip enhancement. They don't affect muscle movement at all.

## Matching Treatment to Concern

If your main concern is expression lines that appear when you smile, frown, or raise your eyebrows, Botox is the more directly relevant treatment. If your concern is more about hollowing, lost volume, or wanting more definition in a specific area, fillers are the better fit. Many patients benefit from a combination, addressing both kinds of concerns where both are present.

## Why This Distinction Matters for Expectations

Using the wrong treatment for a given concern doesn't just waste money -- it can also mean the actual issue goes unaddressed. Under-eye hollowing, for instance, isn't something Botox meaningfully improves, and expression lines caused by muscle movement won't be softened by adding volume alone.

## A Conservative Approach to Both

For both treatments, especially in areas like the lips and under-eyes where overcorrection is most visible, we favor a [conservative starting approach](/blog/botox-natural-results) and build gradually based on how the initial treatment looks, rather than aiming for a dramatic change in a single visit.

## What a Consultation Looks Like

We start by listening to what bothers you, then examine your face at rest and in movement. We discuss which concerns suit which treatment, and which are better addressed in other ways, for example with [skin rejuvenation](/services/skin-rejuvenation) for texture and tone. We also review your medical history, medicines and any previous treatments, because these affect safety.

## How Long Results Last

Botox effects typically build over several days and fade over a few months, so treatment is repeated if you wish to maintain the result. Fillers vary depending on the product and the area, and some last longer than others. Neither is permanent, and we can adjust the plan if your preferences change.

## Possible Side Effects

Common effects include mild swelling, redness and small bruises at the injection sites, which settle in days. Rarely, more significant problems can occur, which is why treatment is done by trained doctors using appropriate products and techniques. Tell us about any bleeding disorders, allergies, pregnancy or breastfeeding, and avoid blood-thinning supplements unless prescribed.

## Choosing a Provider

Ask who will perform the treatment, what product will be used, and what happens if you are not happy with the result. A good provider will happily explain the plan and will not pressure you into extra treatments.
