Finasteride and minoxidil act in different ways. One reduces the hormone that shrinks follicles. The other prolongs the growth phase and thickens hair. Used together they complement each other.

## Why Combine

Studies comparing them show that the combination usually gives better results than either alone, with more men achieving stable or improved hair counts. It is a reasonable starting point for many men with progressive loss.

## Building the Plan

We usually start with one or both depending on your stage, preferences and health. Some begin with finasteride alone and add minoxidil at three to six months if needed. Others start together. Minoxidil may be a lotion or a low-dose tablet.

## Reviewing the Plan

Review at three to four months for tolerability, and at six and twelve months with photographs. If there is poor response, we check that doses are taken, and look at other causes before changing treatment.

## Practical Points

- Take medicines at the same time each day.
- Photograph your scalp every three months in the same lighting.
- Do not judge before six months.
- Report side effects early. See [finasteride side effects](/blog/oral-finasteride-for-hair-loss-benefits-and-side-effects) and [oral minoxidil](/blog/oral-minoxidil-for-hair-loss-what-to-know).
