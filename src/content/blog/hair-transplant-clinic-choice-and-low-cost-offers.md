A hair transplant is a surgical procedure, and the clinic you choose matters more than the headline price.

## Who Performs the Procedure

Ask who plans the hairline and who carries out the key steps, and confirm the qualifications of the doctor responsible.

## Is There a Proper Consultation

You should be examined, not quoted from photographs, and told honestly if you are not a good candidate.

## Be Cautious With

Guaranteed results, pressure to book quickly, unusually large graft numbers promised in one session, and unclear plans for follow-up care.

## Aftercare

A good clinic explains aftercare clearly and is reachable if you have concerns afterwards. Price should be compared like for like, including what follow-up is included.
