A quoted number of grafts is only useful if you understand what sits behind it. Here is what to look for.

## What Determines the Number

The area to be covered, the density you hope to achieve, and the density and extent of your donor area all feed into the estimate. It is a planning figure, not a promise.

## Estimates Need an Examination

A number given from photographs alone is a guess. We assess your donor supply and thinning pattern in person before discussing numbers.

## Questions Worth Asking

Ask how the plan prioritises areas, what happens if more coverage is wanted later, and how much donor supply will remain afterwards. A good plan keeps supply in reserve rather than using everything at once.

## Bigger Is Not Always Better

Packing too many grafts into a single session can compromise survival and leaves less for the future. A measured plan usually serves you better over the years.
