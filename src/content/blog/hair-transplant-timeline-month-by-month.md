The most common source of worry after a transplant is not knowing what is normal. The usual timeline looks like this.

## First Days

Expect redness, small scabs, and some swelling. Follow the washing and sleeping instructions you are given, and avoid touching or scratching the area.

## Weeks 2 to 6: Shedding

The transplanted hair shafts commonly shed. This is expected and does not mean the grafts have failed; the follicles remain beneath the skin.

## Months 3 to 6: New Growth

Fine new hairs begin to appear and gradually thicken. Progress varies between individuals.

## Months 9 to 12

Most patients see the bulk of their final result around a year, with continued maturing after that.

Individual timelines differ, so we review your progress at follow-up visits rather than expecting everyone to follow the same schedule.
