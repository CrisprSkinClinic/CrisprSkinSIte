Many people with hidradenitis suppurativa spend years being treated for "boils" before getting the right diagnosis. Knowing the pattern can shorten that journey.

## What It Looks Like

Deep, painful lumps in skin folds such as the armpits and groin, which burst, drain, and return in the same places. Over time, scars and tunnels under the skin can form.

## What It Is Not

It is not an ordinary infection, it is not contagious, and it is not caused by poor hygiene. Over-washing and harsh scrubbing can make it worse, and the shame many patients feel is not deserved.

## Why Diagnosis Matters

Repeated antibiotic courses for each lump rarely solve the underlying inflammation. Recognising the condition lets treatment target the real problem and aim to reduce flares and scarring.

## What Helps

Treatment is stepwise: medicines matched to severity, lifestyle measures such as stopping smoking and reducing friction, procedures for persistent lesions, and targeted options for severe disease.

## When to Come In

If you have recurring painful lumps in the same folds, an evaluation is worthwhile, and earlier is better.
