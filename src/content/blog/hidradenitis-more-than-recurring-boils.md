Many people with [hidradenitis suppurativa](/conditions/hidradenitis-suppurativa) spend years being treated for "boils" before getting the right diagnosis. Knowing the pattern can shorten that journey.

## What It Looks Like

Deep, painful lumps in skin folds such as the armpits and groin, which burst, drain, and return in the same places. Over time, scars and tunnels under the skin can form.

## What It Is Not

It is not an ordinary infection, it is not contagious, and it is not caused by poor hygiene. Over-washing and harsh scrubbing can make it worse, and the shame many patients feel is not deserved.

## Why Diagnosis Matters

Repeated antibiotic courses for each lump rarely solve the underlying inflammation. Recognising the condition lets treatment target the real problem and aim to reduce flares and scarring.

## What Helps

Treatment is stepwise: medicines matched to severity, lifestyle measures such as stopping smoking and reducing friction, procedures for persistent lesions, and [targeted options](/blog/biologics-in-dermatology-explained) for severe disease.

## When to Come In

If you have recurring painful lumps in the same folds, [an evaluation](/services/biologics) is worthwhile, and earlier is better.

## How Doctors Stage It

Hidradenitis is often described in stages, from isolated nodules without tunnels to widespread disease with interconnected tracts and scarring. Staging helps decide the treatment, since mild disease may respond to topical or oral medicines and lifestyle changes, while more severe disease may need stronger approaches.

## Everyday Measures That Help

Wear loose, breathable clothing, avoid shaving or waxing the affected folds during flares, and use gentle cleansers. Weight management and stopping smoking can reduce flares for many people. Do not squeeze or lance lumps at home, which tends to cause more inflammation and scarring.

## Pain, Mood and Support

Living with repeated painful lumps is exhausting, and many people feel embarrassed or low. Please tell us how much it affects sleep, work and relationships, because good care includes pain relief and emotional support, not only treating the skin.

## Linked Conditions

Hidradenitis can occur alongside other conditions such as acne, joint inflammation and metabolic problems, so we ask about related symptoms. For an overview of treatment options for severe disease, see our guide to [biologics in dermatology](/blog/biologics-in-dermatology-explained) and the page on [biologics and advanced therapy](/services/biologics).

## What to Bring to Your Visit

Note which areas are affected, how often lumps appear, what you have tried and any antibiotics you have taken. Photographs taken during a flare are especially helpful, since lumps may have settled by the time you come in. Tell us about any family history, because hidradenitis often runs in families, and about how pain affects your day.
