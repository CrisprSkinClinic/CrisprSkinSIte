When excess hair is more than expected, the aim is to find why. This guide outlines the usual path.

## Tests

Your doctor may check total and free testosterone, DHEAS, 17-hydroxyprogesterone, prolactin and thyroid tests, often taken early in the menstrual cycle, and blood sugar and cholesterol. A pelvic ultrasound may be advised. Many women need only some of these.

## Common Causes

PCOS is by far the most frequent. Less often, non-classical congenital adrenal hyperplasia, thyroid or prolactin problems, medicines, or an unexplained increase in sensitivity of hair follicles to normal androgen levels. Tumours are rare.

## Medical Treatment

Treatment is chosen by your doctor and may include a combined oral contraceptive, an anti-androgen such as spironolactone or cyproterone, and metformin where insulin resistance is present. Eflornithine cream can slow facial hair. These are prescription medicines with real benefits and real cautions.

## Important Cautions

Anti-androgens can harm an unborn baby, so effective contraception is part of treatment. Medicines are not suitable for everyone, and should not be started without assessment.

## How Long

Hair takes months to respond. Expect six to twelve months for a clear difference, and continued treatment to maintain it. Combine it with [laser](/blog/hirsutism-laser-and-medical-treatment-together) to remove existing hair.
