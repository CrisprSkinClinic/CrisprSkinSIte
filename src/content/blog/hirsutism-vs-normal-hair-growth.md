Women have body and facial hair. How much, and how dark, varies a lot with family and background. So when is it more than normal?

## What Hirsutism Means

Hirsutism is coarse, dark, terminal hair growing in a male-like pattern: upper lip, chin, jawline, chest, abdomen, lower back or inner thighs. Fine, pale hair is not hirsutism.

## How Doctors Score It

A scoring system looks at nine body areas and rates hair growth in each. The total, interpreted in the light of your background, tells whether the growth is more than expected. It is simple, and a more useful measure than how bothered you feel.

## Signs That Make It More Likely to Be Medical

- New hair, or a clear increase over months.
- Irregular periods.
- Acne, particularly along the jawline.
- Thinning at the parting or temples.
- A dark, velvety neck.

Together these point towards [PCOS](/blog/pcos-skin-and-hair-signs-checklist).

## When It Is Urgent

Very rapid growth, a deepening voice, enlargement of the clitoris or muscle build-up needs prompt evaluation.

## If It Is Normal for You

Hair that is within your family's pattern needs no medical treatment. [Laser hair reduction](/blog/laser-hair-reduction-expectations) is a cosmetic option. Read about [hirsutism](/conditions/hirsutism) and [unwanted body and facial hair](/conditions/unwanted-body-and-facial-hair).

## The Ferriman-Gallwey Score

Doctors commonly use a scoring system that looks at nine body areas: upper lip, chin, chest, upper and lower abdomen, upper and lower back, upper arms and thighs. Each area is scored from 0 (no terminal hair) to 4 (heavy growth), and the total is interpreted. A score of 8 or more is often used to define hirsutism in women, though the threshold varies with population. A score is a guide, and the clinical picture matters more.

## Normal Variation Across Populations

Women of South Asian, Mediterranean and Middle Eastern background more commonly have darker body and facial hair than women of Northern European or East Asian background, even with normal hormone levels. Your own and your family's baseline matters. Hair that has always been present and has not changed is less concerning than hair that is new or increasing.

## Hirsutism, Hypertrichosis and Virilisation

- **Hirsutism:** male-pattern, androgen-driven hair.
- **Hypertrichosis:** excess hair anywhere, not androgen-dependent, caused by medicines (such as minoxidil or ciclosporin), illnesses or inheritance.
- **Virilisation:** hirsutism with voice deepening, clitoral enlargement, increased muscle, and breast reduction. It signals very high androgen levels and needs prompt investigation, since rare tumours may be involved.

## What Often Causes It

PCOS accounts for the majority. Less often, non-classical congenital adrenal hyperplasia, thyroid or prolactin problems, and medicines. Some women have "idiopathic hirsutism," in which androgens are normal but hair follicles are sensitive.

## Emotional Impact

Excess facial hair can significantly affect self-confidence. Women may spend hours and money on shaving, threading and waxing, and may avoid social situations. Taking it seriously, both as a medical and cosmetic concern, is important. You do not need to justify seeking treatment.

## Hair Removal Methods in the Meantime

- Shaving does not make hair thicker, but needs frequent repetition.
- Threading and waxing remove hair from the root but can cause irritation and ingrown hairs.
- Depilatory creams can irritate, so patch test.
- Eflornithine cream slows facial hair growth, but must be used continuously.
- [Laser hair reduction](/services/laser-hair-reduction) gives longer-lasting reduction.

## When to Book a Visit

If hair is increasing, accompanied by acne, irregular periods or scalp thinning, or causing distress, book an evaluation. See [hirsutism](/conditions/hirsutism) and [hormone tests and treatment](/blog/hirsutism-hormone-tests-and-treatment).
