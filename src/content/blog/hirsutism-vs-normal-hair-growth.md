Women have body and facial hair. How much, and how dark, varies a lot with family and background. So when is it more than normal?

## What Hirsutism Means

Hirsutism is coarse, dark, terminal hair growing in a male-like pattern: upper lip, chin, jawline, chest, abdomen, lower back or inner thighs. Fine, pale hair is not hirsutism.

## How Doctors Score It

A scoring system looks at nine body areas and rates hair growth in each. The total, interpreted in the light of your background, tells whether the growth is more than expected. It is simple, and a more useful measure than how bothered you feel.

## Signs That Make It More Likely to Be Medical

- New hair, or a clear increase over months.
- Irregular periods.
- Acne, particularly along the jawline.
- Thinning at the parting or temples.
- A dark, velvety neck.

Together these point towards [PCOS](/blog/pcos-skin-and-hair-signs-checklist).

## When It Is Urgent

Very rapid growth, a deepening voice, enlargement of the clitoris or muscle build-up needs prompt evaluation.

## If It Is Normal for You

Hair that is within your family's pattern needs no medical treatment. [Laser hair reduction](/blog/laser-hair-reduction-expectations) is a cosmetic option. Read about [hirsutism](/conditions/hirsutism) and [unwanted body and facial hair](/conditions/unwanted-body-and-facial-hair).
