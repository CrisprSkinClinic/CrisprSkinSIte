People starting hair loss treatment often expect either a miracle or nothing. The truth lies between.

## What Works Best

For pattern hair loss, the best-evidenced treatments are finasteride (in men) and minoxidil (in men and women). Used together, they usually do better than either alone.

## What "Working" Means

- **Stopping loss** is the most common and most reliable benefit. Most men on finasteride stabilise.
- **Thickening** of thin hair is common, and regrowth in thinning areas happens in a good number of people.
- **Regrowth in totally bald areas** is unlikely.

## What Affects Results

How early you start, how long you have been losing hair, family history, and above all consistency. Missing doses is the commonest reason for poor results.

## How Long to Judge

Three to six months for early signs, twelve months for a fair verdict. Pictures taken in the same light and angles are more reliable than memory.

## When Medicines Are Not the Answer

Some hair loss is not pattern loss. [Telogen effluvium](/conditions/telogen-effluvium) is treated by finding the cause. [Alopecia areata](/services/alopecia-areata) and scarring alopecia need different treatment. A proper diagnosis comes first.

## Medicines and Surgery

When loss is advanced and stable, a [hair transplant](/services/hair-transplant-fue) can add density, and medicines protect the surrounding hair. See [medicines after a transplant](/blog/medical-therapy-with-hair-transplant).

## What to Do

Book an examination. We stage your loss, record baseline photos, and agree a plan with timelines. See [medical treatment for hair loss](/services/hair-loss-medical-treatment).

## Success Depends on the Diagnosis

Not all hair loss responds to the same treatment. The table below summarises what to expect in common types of loss.

- **Male pattern hair loss:** finasteride and minoxidil slow loss in most and thicken hair in many. Transplant helps when loss is advanced.
- **Female pattern hair loss:** minoxidil is the mainstay, with anti-androgens in selected women. Response is often good in early disease.
- **Telogen effluvium:** usually recovers by itself once the trigger is removed. Medicines rarely needed.
- **Alopecia areata:** treated with steroids, immune-modulating therapies, and increasingly JAK inhibitors. See [alopecia areata: newer treatment options](/blog/alopecia-areata-newer-treatment-options).
- **Scarring alopecia:** treatment aims to stop damage, as lost follicles do not return.

## Measuring Response Properly

Memory is a poor judge of hair density. We recommend standardised photographs of the crown, the vertex, the frontal area and the sides every three months, taken with the same lighting, hair length and angles. In clinic, a dermatoscope can measure hair counts and thickness. Look for improved density, less scalp visible, fewer hairs on the pillow and thicker strands. Do not judge in the first three months.

## Why Some People Respond Poorly

- Starting late, when many follicles are already miniaturised.
- Missing doses or stopping early.
- Wrong diagnosis, for example a mix of pattern loss and shedding from low iron or thyroid disease.
- Unrealistic expectations about density in a smooth, bald scalp.
- Using products that irritate the scalp and trigger inflammation.

## What Else Can Help

Treating deficiencies, [reducing stress and crash dieting](/blog/nutrition-and-hair-loss-iron-vitamin-d-protein), gentle hair care and, in selected people, in-clinic treatments recommended by your dermatologist. Be cautious about unproven treatments, and about clinics that promise guaranteed results.

## When to Consider a Transplant

When loss is stable, the donor area is good, and expectations are realistic. Medicines usually continue alongside. See [hair transplant candidacy](/blog/hair-transplant-candidacy).

## A Fair Summary

Medical treatment will not give you the hair of your twenties, but it can preserve what you have for years and thicken thinning areas. The earlier and more consistently it is used, the better the result. See [medical treatment for hair loss](/services/hair-loss-medical-treatment).
