People starting hair loss treatment often expect either a miracle or nothing. The truth lies between.

## What Works Best

For pattern hair loss, the best-evidenced treatments are finasteride (in men) and minoxidil (in men and women). Used together, they usually do better than either alone.

## What "Working" Means

- **Stopping loss** is the most common and most reliable benefit. Most men on finasteride stabilise.
- **Thickening** of thin hair is common, and regrowth in thinning areas happens in a good number of people.
- **Regrowth in totally bald areas** is unlikely.

## What Affects Results

How early you start, how long you have been losing hair, family history, and above all consistency. Missing doses is the commonest reason for poor results.

## How Long to Judge

Three to six months for early signs, twelve months for a fair verdict. Pictures taken in the same light and angles are more reliable than memory.

## When Medicines Are Not the Answer

Some hair loss is not pattern loss. [Telogen effluvium](/conditions/telogen-effluvium) is treated by finding the cause. [Alopecia areata](/services/alopecia-areata) and scarring alopecia need different treatment. A proper diagnosis comes first.

## Medicines and Surgery

When loss is advanced and stable, a [hair transplant](/services/hair-transplant-fue) can add density, and medicines protect the surrounding hair. See [medicines after a transplant](/blog/medical-therapy-with-hair-transplant).
