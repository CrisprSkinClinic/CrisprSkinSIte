Different areas of the body have hair with different thickness, depth and growth cycles. This changes how many sessions you need, how it feels and what to watch for.

## Upper Lip, Chin and Face

Facial hair is often finer and grows in shorter cycles, so sessions are spaced about four to six weeks apart, and six to eight are common. In women, facial hair can be hormonal, and it tends to return more readily if the cause is not treated. Pain is mild to moderate. Avoid threading and waxing, and protect the face from the sun.

## Underarms

Coarse, dark hair responds very well, and results are often seen after the third or fourth session. Six to eight sessions are usual. Darker underarm skin needs careful settings. Skip deodorant for a day or two afterwards.

## Bikini Line and Intimate Areas

The skin is darker and more sensitive, so settings are cautious and sessions may take longer. Six to eight sessions are common. The area is more tender, and numbing cream may be used. Choose a clinic with trained, professional staff and suitable privacy.

## Arms and Legs

Large areas, with fine to medium hair that cycles slowly. Sessions are spaced eight to ten weeks apart, and six to eight are common. Results are often good, and fine hair may respond more slowly.

## Back, Chest and Abdomen

Hair is coarse and can be dense in men. These are treated in several sessions spaced about eight to ten weeks, and more may be needed. Ingrown hairs and folliculitis often improve with laser.

## Neck and Hairline

Beard and neck hair, including shaping a neckline, respond well. Men with beard-area hair that is thick need careful planning to avoid thinning the beard unevenly.

## Areas We Treat With Care

Laser is avoided over tattoos and moles, and is used with caution on the nipples and genitals. It is not used in the eye area, and not on skin with active infection or irritation.

## How Pain Is Managed

Most people feel a quick snap or sting. Cooling devices on the laser, numbing creams where suitable, and shorter pulses help. Pain is usually less after the first session, as hair thins.

## How Many Sessions Overall?

Most people need six to eight, and some need more, especially with hormonal hair, light hair or dense hair. We review after the third session and give a realistic estimate.
