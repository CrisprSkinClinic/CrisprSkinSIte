Laser and light treatments are widely offered for skin tone and pigment. They can help, but on brown skin they need the right device and careful settings.

## What These Treatments Are

- **Low-fluence Q-switched Nd:YAG ("laser toning"):** gentle pulses that break up pigment over a series of sessions, used for dullness, mild pigmentation and melasma-type patches in selected people.
- **Intense pulsed light (IPL):** broad light used for sun spots and redness. It is less suited to darker skin because it can burn or cause pigment changes.
- **Vascular lasers:** target tiny blood vessels, used for redness, and selected red marks.
- **Fractional lasers:** resurface the skin for texture. See [fractional laser for acne scars](/blog/fractional-laser-for-acne-scars-recovery-and-results).

## Who Benefits

People with sun spots, a dull or uneven tone, post-acne marks and mild melasma who have already optimised sun protection and creams. Lasers do not replace sunscreen.

## Risks on Brown Skin

The main risk is post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation, a darker patch caused by heat and irritation. In melasma, repeated or high-energy treatments can make it worse and cause a pale patchy look (mottled hypopigmentation). These risks rise with tanning, a poor device choice and frequent sessions.

## Making It Safer

- Choose a clinic where a dermatologist assesses the diagnosis first, because the wrong treatment for the wrong condition worsens it.
- Use low energy and gentle settings, with sessions spaced weeks apart.
- Test a patch first.
- Strict daily sun protection before, during and after.
- Prepare the skin with brightening creams if advised.
- Stop if the skin becomes irritated, and report colour change early.

## What to Expect

Gradual improvement over four to eight sessions, not overnight change. Maintenance sessions and home care keep results.

Chemical peels, prescription creams and oral medicine may suit some patterns of pigmentation better than lasers. See [chemical peels for pigmentation on Indian skin](/blog/chemical-peels-for-pigmentation-and-melasma-on-indian-skin) and our [skin rejuvenation service](/services/skin-rejuvenation).
