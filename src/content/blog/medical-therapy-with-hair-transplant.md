Many people assume a transplant ends their hair loss. It does not. Understanding why helps you plan properly.

## What a Transplant Does

It moves donor hair, which is resistant to pattern loss, into thinning areas. The transplanted hair typically keeps its resistance.

## What It Does Not Do

It does not stop the surrounding native hair from continuing to thin. Over the years, this can leave a transplanted area standing apart from untreated surroundings.

## The Role of Medical Therapy

Medical treatments that slow progression can help preserve your existing hair and keep the overall result looking natural. Whether and which option suits you is a decision made at consultation, considering your age, pattern, and health.

## A Long-Term View

The best outcomes combine a well-planned procedure with a sustainable plan for the hair you still have.
