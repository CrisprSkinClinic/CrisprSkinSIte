Microneedling is a popular treatment for acne scars and skin texture. Done by a trained clinician with the right device, it is a safe, well-studied option, with modest and gradual results.

## What It Is

A device with fine sterile needles makes many tiny controlled channels in the skin. The skin repairs these, producing new collagen over the following weeks, which gradually softens shallow scars and smooths texture. Needle depth is set for the area and the scar, usually between one and two and a half millimetres on the face.

## Who It Suits

- Shallow boxcar scars and rolling scars, often combined with subcision.
- General roughness, enlarged pores and uneven texture.
- Skin of colour, where it carries a lower risk of pigment change than many lasers, although care is still needed.

It does not do much for deep icepick scars, which need TCA CROSS or surgical methods. See [acne scar treatments compared](/blog/acne-scar-treatments-by-scar-type-compared).

## The Session

- The skin is cleaned and a numbing cream applied for 30 to 45 minutes.
- The device is moved over the skin in several passes, which takes about 20 to 30 minutes.
- The skin looks pink or red, and feels like a mild sunburn.
- A soothing, non-irritating cream is applied.

## Recovery

- **Day 1 to 2:** redness, tightness and slight swelling.
- **Day 3 to 5:** dryness and flaking, as the skin renews.
- **Day 5 to 7:** back to normal for most people.

During recovery, avoid the sun, makeup for 24 hours, active ingredients such as retinoids and acids for several days, and swimming, saunas and heavy exercise for a day or two.

## Results and Number of Sessions

Most people need three to six sessions, spaced four to six weeks apart. Improvement builds slowly, with the best result about three months after the last session. Expect a modest improvement, often thirty to fifty percent in suitable scars.

## Who Should Avoid It

- Active acne, skin infection or cold sores on the area.
- A tendency to keloids.
- Recent isotretinoin (we advise waiting some months).
- Pregnancy, and some immune conditions.
- Sunburn or very recent tanning.

## Risks

Redness that lasts, infection, pinpoint bleeding, pigment change and, rarely, scarring from deep needling or unclean devices. Use single-use needle cartridges and a clinic with proper hygiene.

## Home Devices

Dermarollers and home devices cannot safely reach the depth that treats scars, and can cause infection and damage. We do not advise them.
