Every mole removal leaves a mark. How visible it is depends on the method, the site, your skin and how you look after the wound.

## What to Expect by Method

- **Shave removal:** a flat, round pale, pink or slightly darker mark, with no stitches. Mild dip or colour difference is possible.
- **Punch excision:** a small round or short line scar, usually with one or two stitches.
- **Elliptical excision:** a thin line scar longer than the original mole, about three times its length, lying along a skin line.

Scars on the face usually heal best. Scars on the chest, shoulders, back and over joints heal worse, with a greater chance of widening or thickening, especially in darker skin and in keloid-prone people.

## Aftercare: First Days

1. Keep the dressing on for the time advised, often 24 hours, then change as instructed.
2. Clean gently with water or saline, and apply the ointment given.
3. Keep the area dry for the first day, and avoid soaking in baths and pools for a week or so.
4. Do not pick scabs or stitches.
5. Avoid heavy exercise or stretching of the area.
6. Take painkillers if needed.

## Stitches

Stitches are removed after about five to seven days on the face and ten to fourteen days on the body. Following up on time gives a finer scar.

## From Two Weeks to Six Months

- **Protect from the sun:** use sunscreen or cover the area for at least six to twelve months, because new scars darken easily.
- **Use silicone gel or tape** if advised, to soften and flatten the scar.
- **Massage gently** once healed, if your doctor advises.
- **Be patient.** A scar looks worst at one to three months, then fades.

## Scars That Heal Badly

A scar that becomes raised, red, itchy or wide may be hypertrophic or a keloid. Early treatment, such as steroid injections, works far better than waiting. See [scar revision](/services/scar-revision) and [keloid treatment](/blog/keloid-treatment-what-works-and-why-keloids-return).

## Signs of Infection

Increasing pain, redness spreading beyond the wound, pus, heat or fever. Contact us promptly.
