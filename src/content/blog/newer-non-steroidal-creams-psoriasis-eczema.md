Topical corticosteroids remain effective and widely used, but long-term use in sensitive areas needs care. Non-steroidal options have grown in number.

## Why They Matter

Some body areas, such as the face, skin folds, and genitals, are prone to side effects from prolonged steroid use. Non-steroidal creams can help control inflammation in these areas, and can reduce reliance on steroids over time.

## Types Available

These include several classes that act on different inflammatory pathways. Availability, approved uses, and cost differ between products and between countries.

## Where They Fit

They are often used for sensitive sites, for maintenance between flares, or in people who need long-term treatment. They do not replace stronger treatment for severe disease.

## Using Them Well

As with any cream, correct amount, correct site, and consistency matter. Some can cause temporary stinging when first applied.

## What to Do

If you rely on steroid creams, use them often, or worry about long-term use, a review of your plan may identify better options. We can discuss what suits your skin and condition.
