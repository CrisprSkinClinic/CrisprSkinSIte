Good preparation makes the day smoother and supports healing afterwards.

## Tell Us About Your Health

Share your medical history, all medicines and supplements, and any allergies. Some medicines may need to be adjusted before surgery, but never stop prescribed medicines without advice.

## Lifestyle Before the Procedure

We will advise on alcohol, smoking, and certain supplements in the period beforehand, since they can affect bleeding and healing.

## On the Day

Eat beforehand, wear a front-opening top, and arrange for someone to accompany you home if advised.

## Plan Your Recovery

Think about time off work, how you will sleep, and when you will return to normal activities. Having this arranged in advance lowers stress during recovery.
