Many people with psoriasis notice that it gets worse in the cooler months. Several things add up.

## Why Winter Can Make It Worse

Dry air cracks and irritates plaques. Less sunlight means less natural UV, which usually helps psoriasis. Colds and throat infections are common triggers, and so are stress and changes in routine.

## What Helps

- Thick, plain moisturiser to soften scale and reduce itching, applied after bathing.
- Short, lukewarm showers without scrubbing.
- Continuing your prescribed treatment, even when you feel better.
- Treating sore throats promptly, as strep infections can trigger guttate psoriasis in some.
- Managing stress, sleep and alcohol.

## Scalp and Nails

The scalp often itches and flakes more in dry weather. See our page on [scalp psoriasis](/conditions/scalp-psoriasis) and the guide to the [psoriasis treatment ladder](/blog/psoriasis-treatment-ladder).

## When to Step Up Treatment

If plaques are spreading, painful, or affecting joints, tell your doctor. Persistent or widespread disease may need a stronger plan, including [biologics](/services/biologics) in selected patients, and vaccine planning beforehand.
