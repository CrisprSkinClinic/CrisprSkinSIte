Psoriasis is treated in steps, starting simple and moving up only when needed. Understanding the ladder helps you take part in the decision.

## Step One: Topical Treatment

Creams and ointments are the first line for limited disease, and when used correctly they work well. Technique, consistency, and the right strength for the right site matter more than people expect.

## Step Two: Phototherapy

For more widespread disease, supervised light treatment is an established option, given in a series of sessions.

## Step Three: Oral Systemic Medicines

Older oral medicines can control more extensive psoriasis, with monitoring suited to each one.

## Step Four: Biologic Therapy

For moderate-to-severe disease, or disease affecting the joints, targeted injectable medicines may be considered after screening.

## How We Decide

We look at how much skin is involved, which sites are affected, how much it affects your life, other health conditions, and what you have already tried. Moving up a step is a shared decision, and the aim is the simplest plan that keeps you well.
