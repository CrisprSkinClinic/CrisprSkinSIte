Many people who say they have "acne scars" actually have marks, which are flat red or brown patches left behind after spots. They look similar from a distance, but they are different problems, and the treatments are not the same.

## The Difference

**Marks (post-inflammatory erythema and hyperpigmentation):**
- Flat, with no dip or bump when you run a finger over them.
- Red, pink, purple (erythema) or brown (hyperpigmentation).
- They fade over months, with time and sun protection.

**True scars:**
- Change the texture of the skin: pits, dips, bumps or raised areas.
- Visible shadows when light hits the skin from the side.
- Permanent without treatment.

A simple test: stretch the skin. If the mark disappears when stretched, it is probably a flat mark or a shallow depression that may improve. If a pit stays, it is a scar.

## Why Marks Appear

Inflammation from a spot can leave extra pigment (brown marks), or dilated tiny blood vessels (red marks). Picking and squeezing spots worsen both. Indian and other brown skin is prone to brown marks, which can last six to twelve months or longer.

## What Helps Brown Marks

- **Sun protection every day.** Without it, marks stay darker and last much longer. See [do you need sunscreen in winter?](/blog/sunscreen-in-winter-still-needed).
- **Brightening ingredients** such as azelaic acid, niacinamide, vitamin C and, under advice, retinoids and other prescription creams.
- **Gentle chemical peels** in a series. See [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels).
- **Patience.** Even with treatment, fading takes months.

Avoid strong home bleaching creams and mixtures sold without a prescription, which can thin the skin and make colour change worse. See [post-inflammatory hyperpigmentation](/conditions/post-inflammatory-hyperpigmentation).

## What Helps Red Marks

- Sun protection and avoiding picking.
- Azelaic acid and niacinamide.
- Vascular laser (for stubborn red marks) in selected patients, which needs care in darker skin.
- Time. Red marks often fade within three to six months.

## What Helps True Scars

Procedures such as subcision, TCA CROSS, microneedling and laser. See [acne scar treatments compared](/blog/acne-scar-treatments-by-scar-type-compared).

Treat acne early and effectively, do not pick, and use a sunscreen. Good acne control is the best way to prevent both marks and scars. See [acne treatment](/services/acne).
