Rosacea is controlled by treatment and by avoiding the things that set it off. Knowing your triggers and keeping a gentle routine is half the work.

## Common Triggers

- **Sun,** the commonest, and even brief exposure can start a flare.
- **Heat:** hot weather, saunas, hot baths, cooking and overheated rooms.
- **Hot drinks** and **spicy food.**
- **Alcohol,** especially red wine.
- **Emotional stress** and embarrassment.
- **Exercise,** because of rising body temperature.
- **Cold wind** and sudden temperature changes.
- **Skin products:** alcohol-based toners, strong exfoliants, fragrance and menthol.
- **Some medicines** that widen blood vessels.

Triggers differ between people. A simple diary of flares, foods, activities and products for a few weeks shows your own pattern.

## A Gentle Daily Routine

**Morning**
1. Cleanse with lukewarm water and a mild, soap-free cleanser.
2. Apply the treatment your doctor prescribed.
3. Moisturise with a plain, fragrance-free cream.
4. Apply a **mineral (zinc or titanium) sunscreen,** SPF 30 or higher, which is less likely to sting.

**Night**
1. Cleanse gently.
2. Apply the prescribed treatment.
3. Moisturise.

## Things to Avoid

Scrubs, exfoliating brushes, hot water, alcohol-based toners, strong acids and retinoids unless prescribed, fragranced products, and steroid creams on the face.

## Managing Flushing

Cool the face with a cool, damp cloth, sip cool water, move to a cooler place, and carry a hat and sunscreen. Slow breathing helps during stress flushing.

## Makeup

Green-tinted primers can neutralise redness, and mineral makeup is gentle. Remove makeup carefully and gently.
