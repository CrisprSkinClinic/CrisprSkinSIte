Scabies is easy to cure when treatment is done properly, and frustrating when it is not. The usual reasons for failure are applying cream to only part of the body, skipping the repeat dose and not treating family members.

## Confirm the Diagnosis First

Intense itching at night with small bumps and thin burrows between the fingers, wrists, waist, armpits and genitals is typical. Many people are first treated for eczema or allergy. Do not use steroid creams before the diagnosis is made, since they hide signs and let the mites spread. See [scabies](/conditions/scabies).

## Treatment With Permethrin Cream

1. **Bathe** and dry the skin. Trim nails.
2. **Apply the cream** from the neck down to the toes, covering every part: between fingers and toes, under nails, the armpits, groin, genitals, buttocks and the skin folds. In babies, young children and the elderly, also apply to the scalp, face and ears (avoiding the eyes and mouth).
3. **Leave it on** for eight to fourteen hours, usually overnight.
4. **Wash it off** in the morning.
5. **Reapply the whole process after seven days,** since eggs may survive the first treatment.
6. **Wash hands** again with cream if you wash them during treatment.

## Tablets (Ivermectin)

A tablet course is an alternative or an addition, usually two doses a week or two apart, taken with food. It is not suitable in pregnancy, in very small children or in some other situations, so a doctor decides.

## Treat Everyone

All household members and close contacts, including people without itching, must be treated on the same day. See [scabies in families and schools](/blog/scabies-in-families-and-schools-preventing-reinfection).

## Clothes and Bedding

Wash clothes, towels and bedding at the hottest suitable temperature on the day of treatment and dry them in the sun or hot dryer. Items that cannot be washed can be sealed in a bag for several days.

## Why You Still Itch

The mites die within a day, but the skin keeps reacting to dead mites and droppings. Itching and bumps can last two to four weeks. Moisturisers, cool compresses and an antihistamine at night help. A short steroid cream may be used once scabies is treated.

## When Itching Means Treatment Failed

New burrows, new itchy areas, or other people falling ill after two weeks suggest reinfestation or missed contacts. See a doctor for review.

## Crusted Scabies

People with weak immunity can develop thick, crusted skin with huge numbers of mites. It needs specialist care and isolation.
