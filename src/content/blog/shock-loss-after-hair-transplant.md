Some patients notice that not only the transplanted hair sheds, but also some of the existing hair nearby. This is called shock loss.

## Why It Happens

The surgical stress to the area can push nearby existing hairs into a resting phase. It is usually temporary, and most hair regrows over the following months.

## Who Is More Likely to See It

Those transplanting into areas with thin but still-growing native hair are more likely to notice it, which is one reason planning around existing hair is important.

## When to Get Checked

Contact us if shedding is accompanied by pain, spreading redness, discharge, or if the pattern seems very different from what we described at your visit.

## Reassurance

In most cases shock loss resolves without treatment, and understanding it in advance makes the experience far less stressful.
