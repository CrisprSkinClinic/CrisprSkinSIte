Treating the skin is only half the job. Fungus lives in clothes, towels, bedding and shoes, and passes between family members. Fixing the environment prevents relapse.

## Clothing and Towels

- Wash clothes and towels in hot water, and dry them in sun or iron them. Wash during treatment, not only at the end.
- Change underwear and socks daily.
- Choose loose cotton in humid weather, and avoid tight synthetic clothing, especially in the groin.
- Keep your own towel. Do not share, and avoid using the same towel for feet and body.

## Footwear

Rotate shoes so they can dry. Use cotton or moisture-wicking socks, and dry between the toes. Antifungal powder in shoes may help.

## Skin Care

Dry skin folds carefully after bathing. Keep nails short, so scratching does not spread infection. Treat foot or nail fungus at the same time, since it can reinfect the groin and body.

## Family and Pets

Check family members for itchy ring-shaped patches, and treat everyone together. Some ringworm comes from pets, so check them if patches persist.

## Weight, Sweat and Diabetes

Excess sweating, being overweight and diabetes increase recurrence. Controlling blood sugar is part of treatment.

## Gym and Swimming

Wear sandals in showers and pools, and avoid sharing equipment without a barrier.
