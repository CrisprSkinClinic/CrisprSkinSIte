Tinea, or ringworm, is one of the most common skin infections in India. It is also one of the most frequently mismanaged. Most treatment failures come down to a handful of avoidable reasons.

## Why Treatment Fails

1. **Stopping too early.** The itch fades first, and the fungus lingers. Stopping at that point lets it regrow. See [how long to continue](/blog/tinea-how-long-to-continue-treatment).
2. **Steroid or combination creams.** They calm the redness and make it seem to work, but they feed the fungus, and the infection spreads and becomes harder to treat. Read the [warning on steroid creams](/blog/steroid-creams-and-tinea-why-they-make-it-worse).
3. **Applying cream only on the patch.** The fungus extends beyond the visible edge.
4. **Reinfection** from clothes, towels, bedding, shoes and family members.
5. **Wrong diagnosis.** Eczema, psoriasis and other rashes can look like tinea, and the reverse. An examination avoids this.
6. **Resistant organisms.** In recent years, more stubborn fungal strains have appeared in India, which need a doctor's guidance.

## What Good Compliance Looks Like

- Take every dose of tablets, and use the cream as directed, for the full period, even after the skin looks normal.
- Apply cream two to three centimetres beyond the edge.
- Keep skin clean and dry, especially in the groin and folds.
- Wear loose cotton, and wash clothes in hot water and iron them.
- Do not share towels, clothes or combs.
- Treat the whole family if they have patches. See [family and household steps](/blog/tinea-recurrence-prevention-household-and-hygiene).
- Return for review when asked.
