Few skin conditions attract as many food rules as vitiligo. Patients are told to avoid milk, curd, fish, citrus fruit, sour foods or non-vegetarian food together with milk.

## What the Evidence Shows

There is no good evidence that these foods cause vitiligo, or make it spread or worse. Vitiligo is an autoimmune condition, in which the immune system damages pigment cells. Diet is not the cause.

## The Cost of Myths

Cutting out milk, fish or fruit removes protein, calcium, vitamin C and healthy fats, and adds stress. In children, this can affect nutrition.

## What Is Worth Doing

- Eat a balanced diet with plenty of fruit and vegetables.
- Have deficiencies checked, such as vitamin D, B12 and folate, if your doctor advises. Vitiligo is more often seen with thyroid problems, so thyroid testing is often done.
- Protect skin from sunburn, which can trigger new patches.

## Supplements

Antioxidants and vitamin D have been studied, but evidence for helping vitiligo is limited. They are not a replacement for treatment.

## What Does Help

Medical treatment such as topical therapy and narrowband UVB.
