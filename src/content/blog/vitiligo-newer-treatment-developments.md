Vitiligo research has moved quickly, driven by better understanding of the immune processes behind it.

## What Is New

Topical JAK inhibitor creams have shown repigmentation in clinical studies, particularly on the face. They are approved in some countries, while availability and approval elsewhere vary, so it is worth asking what is actually accessible to you.

## What Remains the Foundation

Established treatments such as topical anti-inflammatory therapy and phototherapy remain central, and many people achieve good results with them. Protecting skin from the sun and having realistic expectations about the pace of repigmentation also matter.

## For Stable Vitiligo

When patches have been stable for some time, surgical options for repigmentation may be considered in selected patients.

## Realistic Expectations

Repigmentation is usually slow, taking months, and response differs by body area. Hands and feet tend to respond less well than the face. Vitiligo can recur after treatment stops, so ongoing care is often needed.

## The Emotional Side

Vitiligo can affect confidence and mental health, and acknowledging that is part of good care.

## What to Do

If you have vitiligo, an evaluation lets us stage your disease and discuss which options fit your pattern, rather than relying on what you have read online.
