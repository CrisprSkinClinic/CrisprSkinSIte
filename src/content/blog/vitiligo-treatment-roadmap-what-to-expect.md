Vitiligo treatment is a process, not a single decision. Here is how the pieces usually fit together.

## Step 1: Diagnosis and Classification

Examination, often with a Wood's lamp, confirms the diagnosis and assesses extent, type (segmental or non-segmental), and whether the disease is active or stable. We may screen for associated conditions such as thyroid disease.

## Step 2: Calm Activity if Needed

For active, spreading disease, the first aim is to halt progress, using phototherapy, topical treatment, and in rapidly progressing cases a short supervised course of oral medicine.

## Step 3: Restore Pigment

Topical treatment for limited patches and phototherapy for more extensive disease, continued for months with regular review.

## Step 4: Consider Surgery for Resistant, Stable Patches

Grafting and cell-transfer techniques for patches that have stopped changing but have not responded to medical treatment.

## Step 5: Support and Maintenance

Sun protection, camouflage where wanted, and attention to emotional wellbeing. Follow-up continues because vitiligo can recur.

## Realistic Expectations

Results vary by body area, patch age, and disease activity. We explain this honestly from the start, and the goal is the best result for your pattern, without false promises.
