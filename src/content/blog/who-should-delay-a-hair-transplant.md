A good consultation sometimes ends with advice to wait, and that is a sign of an honest evaluation.

## Very Early or Unstable Hair Loss

In young patients with a pattern that is still changing quickly, a transplant planned too early can leave an unnatural look as loss progresses. Medical therapy and monitoring often come first.

## Unclear Diagnosis

Not all hair loss is pattern baldness. Conditions such as alopecia areata, scarring alopecia, or loss from nutritional or hormonal causes need to be diagnosed and treated first.

## Limited Donor Supply

When the donor area cannot support the coverage a patient wants, we discuss realistic goals rather than overpromising.

## Unrealistic Expectations

A transplant improves density and shape; it cannot restore a teenage hairline. Honest expectations lead to satisfied patients.
