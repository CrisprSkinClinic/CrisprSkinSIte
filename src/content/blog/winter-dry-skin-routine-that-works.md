When humidity drops, skin loses water faster. In Chennai, the trigger is often air-conditioning, cooler mornings or travel to colder places. The fix is simple, and it is mostly about habits.

## Bathe Smarter

Use lukewarm, not hot, water, and keep showers to about ten minutes. Use a gentle, fragrance-free cleanser only where needed, not over the whole body.

## Moisturise Within Minutes

Pat dry, and apply moisturiser while the skin is still slightly damp. Thick creams and ointments hold moisture better than thin lotions. Look for ceramides, glycerin, petrolatum or urea.

## Reapply on Problem Areas

Hands, shins, elbows and heels need more. Moisturise after washing your hands, and consider a rich hand cream at night.

## Humidity and Clothing

Do not sit with air-conditioning blowing directly on you. Cotton next to the skin is kinder than wool. Wash clothes with a mild, fragrance-free detergent and rinse well.
