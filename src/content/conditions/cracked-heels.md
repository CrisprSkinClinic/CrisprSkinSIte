Heel skin is thick and has few oil glands, so it dries easily. Pressure from standing spreads the heel and splits the dry skin. Treat it early, before cracks are deep.

Our guide on [cracked heels and chapped lips in winter](/blog/cracked-heels-and-chapped-lips-in-winter) has a step-by-step routine. People with diabetes should have any heel crack looked at promptly.
