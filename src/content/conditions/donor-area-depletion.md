Hair transplantation moves hair from the back and sides of the scalp, which is largely resistant to pattern loss, to thinning areas. The supply is finite, so no one can be given unlimited density.

Good planning concentrates grafts where they matter most, such as the frontal hairline and forelock, and accepts that coverage is a trade-off against supply. Protecting remaining native hair with medical therapy stretches the benefit of the donor hair you have.
