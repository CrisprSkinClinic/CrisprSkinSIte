Hirsutism means excess coarse, dark hair in women in areas that usually grow hair in men. It is different from fine, light body hair, which is normal. Most often it reflects raised androgen activity, and in the large majority of cases, the cause is PCOS.

Our guides cover [how to tell hirsutism from normal hair](/blog/hirsutism-vs-normal-hair-growth), [tests and medical treatment](/blog/hirsutism-hormone-tests-and-treatment), and [how laser and medicines work together](/blog/hirsutism-laser-and-medical-treatment-together).

## Medicine and Laser Together

Laser removes the hair that is present. Medicines treat why it keeps coming. Doing only one is the commonest reason for disappointment. See [laser hair reduction](/services/laser-hair-reduction) and [unwanted body and facial hair](/conditions/unwanted-body-and-facial-hair).

## Pregnancy

Several treatments for hirsutism are not safe in pregnancy, so reliable contraception is part of the plan, or the treatment is chosen accordingly.
