Human papillomavirus is a large family of viruses. A few types cause genital warts. A smaller group of high-risk types causes cervical cancer and some cancers of the vulva, anus and throat. The common warts on hands and feet are caused by different types, and the HPV vaccine is not meant for those.

See our guide to [what the HPV vaccine prevents and what it does not](/blog/hpv-vaccine-what-it-prevents-and-what-it-does-not), and our page on [viral warts](/conditions/viral-warts) for ordinary warts.

## Who Should Consider the Vaccine

Girls and boys from 9 years, young adults, and some older adults after a discussion. People about to start immunosuppressants are encouraged to complete the course first where possible. See our [vaccination service](/services/vaccination).
