Psoriasis is considered moderate-to-severe when it covers a large area, involves difficult sites such as the [scalp](/conditions/scalp-psoriasis), [nails](/conditions/nail-psoriasis), or genitals, causes significant distress, or fails to respond to topical treatment. Severity is judged by the extent of skin involved and by how much the condition affects daily life, not just by appearance.

Treatment [steps up gradually](/blog/psoriasis-treatment-ladder): phototherapy, established oral medicines, and, where appropriate, [biologic therapy](/blog/biologics-in-dermatology-explained). The right option depends on your health, other conditions, and goals, and [safety screening](/services/biologics) comes first. The aim is long-term control, since psoriasis is a chronic condition rather than one that is cured.

## When Psoriasis Needs More Than Creams

Psoriasis is moderate to severe when it covers a large area, affects the face, scalp, hands, feet, genitals or nails, causes significant distress, or does not respond to topical treatment and light therapy. Joint involvement also moves it into this category.

## Assessment

A doctor measures the extent and severity, checks for joint symptoms and associated conditions such as obesity, high cholesterol, diabetes and fatty liver, and reviews medicines and infections. Baseline blood tests and screening for tuberculosis and hepatitis B and C are done before most systemic treatments.

## Treatment Options

- **Phototherapy (narrowband UVB).**
- **Methotrexate, acitretin or ciclosporin** tablets, with monitoring.
- **Biologics:** injectable medicines targeting specific signals in psoriasis, effective in many people, including those with joint disease.
- **Small-molecule targeted tablets** for selected patients.

Choice depends on your health, pregnancy plans, other illnesses, previous treatments, convenience and cost. See [biologics and advanced therapy](/services/biologics) and [biologics in dermatology explained](/blog/biologics-in-dermatology-explained).

## Safety and Vaccines

Medicines that lower immunity call for infection precautions and vaccine planning, ideally before starting. See [vaccines before immunosuppressants](/blog/vaccines-before-immunosuppressants-biologics-checklist) if available.

## Whole-Person Care

Heart health, weight, sleep, mood and alcohol matter as much as the skin. See [psoriasis](/services/psoriasis).
