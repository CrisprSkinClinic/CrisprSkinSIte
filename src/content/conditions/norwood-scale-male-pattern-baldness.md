The Norwood scale grades male pattern hair loss from stage 1, with little or no loss, to stage 7, extensive loss across the front and crown. It gives doctors and patients a shared language for how far loss has progressed.

Stage matters because hair loss is usually progressive. Planning a transplant around a young, rapidly changing pattern needs care, which is why medical therapy and a longer-term view are discussed alongside surgery.
