Photoageing refers to skin changes driven mainly by years of sun exposure, rather than by the passage of time alone. It shows up as fine lines, uneven tone, sun spots, and rougher texture, and in deeper skin tones often appears as [patchy pigmentation](/conditions/post-inflammatory-hyperpigmentation) more than as wrinkles.

Because so much of it is [sun-related](/blog/sunscreen-and-visible-light-indian-skin), prevention is both the simplest and most effective measure. Established changes can be improved with a combination of [topical treatment](/blog/retinol-vs-retinoids-how-to-start) and [in-clinic options](/services/anti-ageing), planned to suit your skin type and the time you can set aside for recovery.

## What Photoageing Is

Photoageing is skin ageing caused by long-term exposure to ultraviolet light, on top of natural ageing. It accounts for most visible ageing of the face, neck, chest and hands.

## Signs

Fine lines and deeper wrinkles, rough or leathery texture, uneven colour with sun spots (lentigines), freckling, broken blood vessels, loss of firmness and, in some, precancerous rough patches. In brown skin, the commonest signs are uneven pigmentation, dullness and texture changes, with wrinkles appearing later.

## Prevention: What Works Best

1. **Daily broad-spectrum sunscreen,** SPF 30 or higher, reapplied outdoors. For brown skin, tinted sunscreen also blocks visible light. See [sunscreen for Indian skin](/blog/sunscreen-and-visible-light-indian-skin).
2. **Shade, hats and sunglasses** during peak sun hours.
3. **Not smoking.**
4. **A retinoid at night,** which is the best-evidenced anti-ageing cream, started slowly. See [retinol and retinoids: how to start](/blog/retinol-vs-retinoids-how-to-start).
5. **Antioxidant serum** such as vitamin C in the morning for added protection, though evidence is more modest.

## Treatments

Chemical peels for tone and texture, laser and light treatments for pigment and vessels, and neuromodulators and fillers for lines and volume. See [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels), [Botox](/services/botox) and [fillers](/services/fillers). Skin cancer risk rises with sun damage, so any new, changing or non-healing spot should be examined.

## Realistic Goals

Treatment can soften signs, but protection and consistency matter more than any single procedure. See [anti-ageing](/services/anti-ageing).
