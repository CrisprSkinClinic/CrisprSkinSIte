In scarring alopecia, inflammation destroys the hair follicle and replaces it with scar tissue, so affected hair does not return. The aim of treatment is to stop progression and protect remaining hair.

This matters for transplant candidates because a transplant is generally considered only after the disease has been quiet for a period and the diagnosis is secure. Proper assessment comes first.
