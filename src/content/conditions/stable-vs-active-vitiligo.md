Vitiligo does not behave the same way in every person, or even at all times in the same person. Active disease means pigment loss is still progressing, with new or enlarging patches. Stable disease means it has settled, usually judged over six to twelve months without change.

Knowing which phase you are in changes the plan. Active vitiligo needs the immune process calmed first, and surgery is avoided because new patches can appear in treated skin. In stable disease, the focus can move to restoring pigment, including surgical options where medical treatment has not been enough.
