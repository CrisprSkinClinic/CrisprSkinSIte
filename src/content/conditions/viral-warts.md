Viral warts are caused by a [viral infection](/blog/viral-skin-infections-school) of the skin and are contagious, spreading through direct contact or shared surfaces. They can appear anywhere on the body but are especially common on the hands and feet, sometimes clustering together over time if untreated.

[Cryotherapy](/services/warts-moles-skin-tags) -- freezing the wart with liquid nitrogen -- is the standard first-line treatment, typically requiring a few sessions spaced a few weeks apart depending on size and location. Warts that don't respond adequately to cryotherapy can be managed with alternative removal approaches discussed at your evaluation. Another common viral skin infection with a similar course is [molluscum contagiosum](/conditions/molluscum-contagiosum).

## What Causes Warts

Warts are skin growths caused by human papillomavirus (HPV), spread by direct contact or from shared surfaces such as bathroom floors and towels. Cuts and wet, soft skin help the virus in. The types that cause common warts on the hands and feet differ from those behind genital warts.

## Types

- **Common warts:** rough, raised, on the fingers and hands.
- **Plantar warts (verrucae):** on the soles, pressed in by body weight and often painful, with tiny black dots.
- **Flat warts:** smooth, flat-topped, in groups on the face, hands and legs.
- **Filiform warts:** thread-like, on the face and neck.
- **Genital warts:** a separate topic. See [HPV infection](/conditions/hpv-infection) if it applies.

## Treatment Is Not Always Needed

Many warts, especially in children, clear on their own over months to two years. Treatment is advised for painful, spreading or unsightly warts and in people with weak immunity.

## Treatment Options

- **Salicylic acid** paste or patches, applied daily for weeks, with gentle filing.
- **Cryotherapy** (freezing), repeated every two to three weeks.
- **Cautery and other minor procedures** for stubborn warts.
- **Other prescription treatments** for resistant or large warts.

Warts return in some people, and persistence is needed. Avoid home cutting, which spreads warts.

## Prevention

Cover warts, do not pick, keep feet dry, wear sandals in communal showers and do not share towels. The HPV vaccine does not prevent common warts.
