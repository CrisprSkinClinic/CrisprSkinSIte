---
question: "Are there newer treatments for severe alopecia areata?"
tags: ["alopecia-areata", "dermatology-updates"]
---

Yes, for selected patients with extensive or persistent disease, newer targeted medicines such as JAK inhibitors have shown regrowth in clinical studies. They need assessment and monitoring, and availability differs between medicines. Many people with patchy disease still do well with established treatments, so the right option depends on your pattern and history.
