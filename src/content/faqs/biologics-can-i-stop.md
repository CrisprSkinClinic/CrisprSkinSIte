---
question: "Can I stop a biologic once my skin clears?"
tags: ["biologics", "psoriasis", "atopic-dermatitis"]
---

For chronic conditions such as psoriasis and atopic dermatitis, disease often returns after stopping, because the medicine controls rather than cures. Any change to your treatment, including stopping, spacing doses, or switching, should be planned with your doctor rather than done on your own.
