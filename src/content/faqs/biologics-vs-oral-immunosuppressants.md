---
question: "How are biologics different from older oral immune-suppressing medicines?"
tags: ["biologics", "psoriasis", "atopic-dermatitis"]
---

Older oral medicines act broadly on the immune system or on inflammation, and often need regular blood monitoring for effects on the liver, kidneys, or blood counts. Biologics target one specific pathway, which can mean fewer organ-related side effects, though they carry their own considerations such as infection screening. The right choice depends on your condition, other health issues, and what has already been tried.
