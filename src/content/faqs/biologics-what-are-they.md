---
question: "What are biologic medicines in dermatology?"
tags: ["biologics", "psoriasis", "atopic-dermatitis", "urticaria"]
---

Biologics are medicines made from living cells that block one specific signal in the immune system, rather than suppressing it broadly. They are typically given by injection and are reserved for moderate-to-severe skin disease that has not responded adequately to topical treatment, phototherapy, or other standard options.
