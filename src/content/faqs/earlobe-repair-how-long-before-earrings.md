---
question: "How long after earlobe repair can I wear earrings again?"
tags: ["earlobe-repair"]
---

Healing needs time, commonly several weeks to a few months, until the scar is soft, flat, and settled. Piercing too early can reopen the repair. We will tell you when it is safe, and usually advise placing the new piercing slightly away from the scar line and using lightweight earrings.
