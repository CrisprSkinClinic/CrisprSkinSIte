---
question: "I get keloids. Can I still have my earlobe repaired?"
tags: ["earlobe-repair"]
---

Possibly, but it needs a careful plan, because any wound on the earlobe, including a repair, can trigger a keloid in people who are prone to them. Tell us about any past keloids, particularly from ear piercings. We discuss the risks, whether repair is advisable, and the aftercare that lowers the chance of a thick scar.
