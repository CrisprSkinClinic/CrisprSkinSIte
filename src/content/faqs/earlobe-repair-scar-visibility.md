---
question: "Will earlobe repair leave a visible scar?"
tags: ["earlobe-repair"]
---

A fine scar remains, but it is usually small and fades over months, often becoming hard to notice, particularly because the lobe is soft and the scar sits at the edge. Shaped closures help break up the line. Scarring is more likely to be prominent in people who form thick scars or keloids, which we plan for in advance.
