---
question: "Is oral minoxidil safe for hair loss?"
tags: ["hair-medicines"]
---

Low-dose oral minoxidil is generally well tolerated when prescribed carefully, but it is used off-label and is not for everyone. Side effects include extra facial and body hair, ankle swelling, a fast heartbeat and dizziness. It is not suitable for some people with heart or blood pressure problems, or in pregnancy, so it needs a medical check and follow-up.
