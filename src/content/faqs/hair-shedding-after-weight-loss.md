---
question: "Why am I losing hair after losing weight quickly?"
tags: ["hair-loss", "dermatology-updates"]
---

Rapid weight loss and reduced intake can push many hairs into a resting phase, causing diffuse shedding a few months later. It usually settles and regrows once nutrition and weight stabilise. If shedding is heavy or prolonged, we check for other contributors such as iron deficiency, thyroid problems, or pattern hair loss.
