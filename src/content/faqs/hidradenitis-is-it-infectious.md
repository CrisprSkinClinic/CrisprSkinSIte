---
question: "Is hidradenitis suppurativa an infection, and is it contagious?"
tags: ["hidradenitis-suppurativa", "acne"]
---

No. It is a chronic inflammatory condition of the hair follicles, not an infection caught from someone else, and it cannot be passed on by contact. It is also not caused by poor hygiene. Bacteria may be present in the lumps, but they are not the root cause, which is why repeated antibiotics for "boils" often provide only temporary relief.
