---
question: "What are the treatment options for hidradenitis suppurativa?"
tags: ["hidradenitis-suppurativa", "acne"]
---

Treatment is matched to severity. Mild disease may be managed with topical and oral medicines and lifestyle measures such as stopping smoking and reducing friction. More severe disease may need targeted medicines, and persistent scarred areas can be treated with procedures. Your doctor will outline a stepwise plan after examining you.
