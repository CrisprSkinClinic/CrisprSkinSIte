---
question: "When should I see a doctor for recurring boils in the armpits or groin?"
tags: ["hidradenitis-suppurativa", "acne"]
---

If boil-like lumps keep returning in the same folds, take weeks to settle, leave scars, or drain repeatedly, it is worth an evaluation rather than treating each one as a one-off. Earlier diagnosis usually means better control and less scarring. Seek prompt care for fever, rapidly spreading redness, or severe pain.
