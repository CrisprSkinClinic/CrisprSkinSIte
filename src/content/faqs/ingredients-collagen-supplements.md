---
question: "Do collagen supplements work for skin?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "anti-ageing"]
---

The evidence is limited and mixed. Some small studies suggest modest improvements in skin hydration or elasticity, but many are short and not independent, and the supplement is digested rather than delivered intact to the skin. If you choose to take one, view it as an optional extra with modest expectations. Sun protection and a sound skincare routine remain the most reliable ways to protect collagen.
