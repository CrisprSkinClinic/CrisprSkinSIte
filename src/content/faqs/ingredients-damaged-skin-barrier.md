---
question: "How do I know if I have damaged my skin barrier, and what should I do?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "eczema"]
---

Signs include stinging with products that used to be fine, tightness, redness, flaking, and sudden sensitivity, often after over-exfoliating or using too many strong actives. Pause all actives and exfoliants, use a gentle cleanser, a plain moisturizer, and sunscreen, and give skin a few weeks to recover. See us if it does not settle, as eczema or contact dermatitis can look similar.
