---
question: "In what order should I apply skincare products?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients"]
---

A simple guide is to go from thinnest to thickest: cleanser, then treatment serums, then moisturizer, and in the morning sunscreen last. Keep the routine short, introduce one new active at a time, and avoid combining several strong actives on the same evening unless advised. More products do not mean better results.
