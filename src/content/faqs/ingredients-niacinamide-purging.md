---
question: "Can niacinamide cause breakouts or purging?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "acne"]
---

Niacinamide does not typically cause true "purging", which is more characteristic of ingredients that speed up skin turnover. If breakouts appear after starting it, the cause may be another product in the routine, a heavy formulation, or a reaction to the product itself. Stop adding new products, and see us if the problem persists beyond a few weeks.
