---
question: "Is a higher-strength niacinamide better? Is 10% too much?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "acne"]
---

Not necessarily. Benefits are seen at modest concentrations, and higher strengths do not reliably work better but can irritate some skin. Many people do well with a lower to moderate strength. If you have sensitive or reactive skin, begin low, use it every other day at first, and increase only if your skin tolerates it.
