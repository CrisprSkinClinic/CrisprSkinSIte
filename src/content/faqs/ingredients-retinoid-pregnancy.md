---
question: "Are retinoids safe during pregnancy or breastfeeding?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "acne"]
---

Retinoids are generally avoided during pregnancy and while trying to conceive, and your doctor will advise on use while breastfeeding. If you are using one and think you may be pregnant, stop it and tell your doctor. Safer alternatives exist for acne and pigmentation during pregnancy, and we can help you choose them.
