---
question: "How should I start a retinoid without my skin peeling and burning?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "acne", "anti-ageing"]
---

Start low and slow: a small amount, two or three evenings a week, on dry skin, and build up as tolerated. Pair it with a plain moisturizer and use sunscreen every morning, since skin is more sun-sensitive. Some dryness and flaking in the first weeks is common. Persistent burning, swelling, or severe peeling means you should stop and check with us.
