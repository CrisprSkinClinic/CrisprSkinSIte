---
question: "What is the difference between retinol and prescription retinoids?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "acne", "anti-ageing"]
---

Both belong to the vitamin A family. Over-the-counter retinol must be converted by the skin into the active form, so it is generally gentler and slower. Prescription retinoids are stronger and used for conditions such as acne and for more significant ageing changes. Which is right for you depends on your skin, your goals, and how well you tolerate them.
