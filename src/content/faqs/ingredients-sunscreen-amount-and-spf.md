---
question: "Which sunscreen should I use, and how much do I need?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "pigmentation", "melasma"]
---

Choose a broad-spectrum sunscreen with SPF 30 or higher in a texture you will actually use every day. Most people apply far too little. A useful guide for the face and neck is roughly two finger-lengths of product, reapplied every two to three hours when outdoors. For melasma or stubborn pigmentation, a tinted sunscreen that also helps block visible light is often preferred.
