---
question: "Do I need sunscreen indoors or on cloudy days?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "pigmentation", "melasma"]
---

Daylight reaches you through windows and on cloudy days, so people prone to pigmentation or melasma generally benefit from daily sunscreen even when mostly indoors. If you spend the day away from windows and outdoor light, the need is lower. When in doubt, a daily morning application is a simple habit that costs little.
