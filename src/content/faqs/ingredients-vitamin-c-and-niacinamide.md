---
question: "Can I use vitamin C and niacinamide together?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "pigmentation"]
---

For most people, yes. The old advice that they cancel each other out is largely outdated, and many products combine them. If your skin becomes irritated, use them at different times of day, for example vitamin C in the morning and niacinamide at night, and introduce one at a time so you can tell which is responsible for any reaction.
