---
question: "Does vitamin C serum actually work, and how should I use it?"
tags: ["skincare-ingredients", "pigmentation", "anti-ageing"]
---

Topical vitamin C is an antioxidant that can help protect skin from sun-related damage, support a brighter-looking tone, and modestly help with dark marks over time. Results are gradual, over weeks to months. It works as a supporting step alongside sunscreen, not a replacement for it. Stability and formulation matter, so choose a well-packaged product, start with a lower strength if your skin is sensitive, and stop if it causes persistent stinging or redness.
