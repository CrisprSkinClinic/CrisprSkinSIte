---
question: "What are JAK inhibitors, and are they biologics?"
tags: ["biologics", "alopecia-areata", "atopic-dermatitis", "vitiligo"]
---

JAK inhibitors are not biologics. They are small-molecule medicines, usually taken as tablets or applied as creams, that block signalling inside immune cells. They are used for certain conditions such as severe atopic dermatitis and alopecia areata, depending on the specific medicine and its approved uses. They need pre-treatment assessment and ongoing monitoring, and your doctor will advise whether one is appropriate for you.
