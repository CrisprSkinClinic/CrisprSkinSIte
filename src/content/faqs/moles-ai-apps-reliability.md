---
question: "Can a skin-checking app tell me if a mole is safe?"
tags: ["moles", "dermatology-updates"]
---

No. Apps vary in quality, and photos are affected by lighting, skin tone, and location, so a reassuring result can mislead. Treat an app as a prompt to see a doctor, not a replacement. Any mole that is changing, new, bleeding, or looks different from your others should be examined in person.
