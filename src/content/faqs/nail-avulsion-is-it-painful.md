---
question: "Is ingrown toenail surgery painful?"
tags: ["nail-avulsion", "ingrown-toenail"]
---

The local anaesthetic injection at the base of the toe is the most uncomfortable part, a brief sharp sting. After that the toe is numb and you should feel only pressure, not pain. Some soreness is normal once the anaesthetic wears off, and simple pain relief and keeping the foot raised usually manage it.
