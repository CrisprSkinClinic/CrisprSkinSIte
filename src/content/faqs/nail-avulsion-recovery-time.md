question: "How long does recovery take after nail avulsion with matricectomy?"
tags: ["nail-avulsion", "ingrown-toenail"]

Most people return to ordinary activities within days, but the toe takes several weeks to heal fully. Expect drainage and tenderness during that time, with dressings and roomy footwear. Strenuous exercise and tight shoes usually wait until healing allows, and we will give you specific timings.
