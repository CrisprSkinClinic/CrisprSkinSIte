---
question: "Can I have ingrown toenail surgery if I have diabetes?"
tags: ["nail-avulsion", "ingrown-toenail"]
---

It may be possible, but it needs a careful assessment first. Diabetes and poor circulation can slow healing and raise the risk of infection, so we check blood sugar control, circulation, and sensation in the foot before deciding. Tell us about your diabetes and medicines at consultation, and contact us early if the toe looks worse after the procedure.
