---
question: "Can an ingrown toenail come back after the procedure?"
tags: ["nail-avulsion", "ingrown-toenail"]
---

Recurrence is much less likely than after simply removing the nail edge, because the growth point is treated as well. However, no procedure removes the chance entirely, and it can happen if some nail-forming tissue survives. Good nail trimming and sensible footwear help protect the result.
