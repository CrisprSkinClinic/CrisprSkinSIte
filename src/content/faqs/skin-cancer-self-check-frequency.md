---
question: "How often should I check my skin for signs of skin cancer?"
tags: ["moles", "dermatology-updates"]
---

A monthly self-check is a sensible habit, covering the scalp, palms, soles, between the toes, and nails, with help for areas you cannot see. If you have many moles, a family history, or have had skin cancer, regular professional examination is advisable. Book a visit promptly for any new or changing lesion.
