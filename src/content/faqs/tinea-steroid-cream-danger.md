---
question: "Why should I avoid steroid creams on ringworm?"
tags: ["fungal"]
---

Steroids reduce redness and itch quickly, so it seems to work, but they feed the fungus. The rash spreads, becomes atypical, and stops responding to antifungals. This is a major reason for stubborn tinea. Do not use a steroid or a combination cream without a doctor's advice.
