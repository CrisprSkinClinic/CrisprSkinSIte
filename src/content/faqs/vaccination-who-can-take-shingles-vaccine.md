---
question: "Who can take the shingles vaccine?"
tags: ["vaccination"]
---

The recombinant (non-live) shingles vaccine is recommended for adults from 50 years, and for adults from 18 years who have weakened immunity or are about to start immunosuppressant treatment. It is given as two doses, usually two to six months apart, and sooner for people with weakened immunity. Your doctor will check that it suits you.
