---
question: "What if vitiligo covers most of my body? Is there a treatment for that?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

When vitiligo is very extensive and repigmentation is not realistic, some patients choose to lighten the remaining pigmented skin so the tone becomes uniform. This is a permanent, carefully considered decision with real implications, including lifelong sun protection, and it is only discussed after other options have been explored and you have had time to think it through.
