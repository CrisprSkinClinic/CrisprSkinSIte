---
question: "Can diet or supplements treat vitiligo?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

No diet reliably causes or cures vitiligo. Some supplements, such as antioxidants and vitamins, have been studied, but evidence of benefit is limited, and they are not a substitute for medical treatment. If you are deficient in something, such as vitamin D or B12, correcting it is sensible for general health. Be cautious about products that promise a cure.
