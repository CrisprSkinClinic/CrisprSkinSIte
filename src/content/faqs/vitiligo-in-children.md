---
question: "How is vitiligo treated in children?"
tags: ["vitiligo", "pediatric"]
---

Treatment in children favours gentler options, usually topical treatment chosen carefully for age and site, along with sun protection and, in older children with extensive disease, supervised phototherapy. Surgery is generally reserved for older patients. Emotional support matters, since vitiligo can affect confidence at school. We work with families to set realistic goals.
