---
question: "How does narrowband UVB phototherapy work for vitiligo?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

Narrowband UVB uses a specific wavelength of ultraviolet light that calms the immune activity in the skin and stimulates remaining pigment cells, including those around hair follicles, to repopulate the patches. It is given in short, supervised sessions, typically two to three times a week, and is a well-established first-line option for more extensive vitiligo. Eye protection is used, and dose is increased carefully to limit burning.
