---
question: "Is there a new cream for vitiligo?"
tags: ["vitiligo", "dermatology-updates"]
---

Topical JAK inhibitor creams have shown repigmentation in studies, mainly on the face, and are approved in some countries. Availability and approval vary, so we will tell you what is practically accessible. Established treatments such as topical therapy and phototherapy remain the foundation, and results take months in any case.
