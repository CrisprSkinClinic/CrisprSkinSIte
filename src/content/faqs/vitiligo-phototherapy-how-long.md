---
question: "How long does vitiligo treatment take to show results?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

Repigmentation is slow. With topical treatment or phototherapy, early signs such as small pigmented dots around hair follicles often appear after a few months, and treatment commonly continues for six months to a year or longer before a judgement is made. We review progress at set intervals, and if a body area shows no response after an adequate trial, we change the plan.
