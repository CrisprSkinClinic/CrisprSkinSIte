---
question: "What is the difference between segmental and non-segmental vitiligo?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

Non-segmental vitiligo is the common type, with patches often appearing symmetrically on both sides of the body and tending to progress and recur. Segmental vitiligo affects one area or segment of the body, usually appears early, spreads for a limited period, and then often stabilises. It tends to respond poorly to medical treatment but well to surgical repigmentation once stable.
