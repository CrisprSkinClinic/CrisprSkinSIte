---
question: "What does stable vitiligo mean, and how is it judged?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

Stable vitiligo means no new patches and no enlargement of existing ones over a period, often judged as six to twelve months, with no recent Koebner phenomenon (new patches at sites of friction or injury). Doctors consider history, serial photographs, and examination features suggesting activity. Stability matters because it guides treatment: active disease needs calming first, while stable disease may be suitable for surgery.
