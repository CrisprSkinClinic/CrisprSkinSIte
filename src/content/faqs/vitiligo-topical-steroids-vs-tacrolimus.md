---
question: "Which cream is better for vitiligo: a steroid or tacrolimus?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

Both calm the local immune activity that destroys pigment cells. Topical steroids are effective for limited patches but are used for short, supervised courses because prolonged use can thin the skin, particularly on the face and skin folds. Tacrolimus and similar calcineurin inhibitors do not cause skin thinning, so they are preferred for the face, neck, and folds and for longer-term use. Your doctor chooses based on the site and extent of your patches.
