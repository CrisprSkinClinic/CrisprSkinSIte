---
question: "What treatment options are available for vitiligo?"
tags: ["vitiligo"]
---

Options include topical therapy (steroids, tacrolimus, and in some places newer topical agents), phototherapy such as narrowband UVB and targeted light, short courses of oral medicine for rapidly spreading disease, and surgical repigmentation for stable patches. Camouflage, micropigmentation, and sun protection are supportive measures. The right combination depends on your type, extent, stability, and the sites involved.
