---
question: "Does xanthelasma mean I have high cholesterol?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

Not always, but it can. In a good proportion of people with xanthelasma, blood lipids are abnormal, while others have normal results. That is why we recommend a fasting lipid profile and a review of your risk factors. Checking is worthwhile, because raised cholesterol often causes no symptoms yet affects long-term heart health.
