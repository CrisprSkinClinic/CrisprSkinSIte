---
question: "Is xanthelasma dangerous or cancerous?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

Xanthelasma themselves are benign. They do not turn cancerous and do not harm the eye or vision. The reason to take them seriously is that they can reflect raised blood lipids, which matter for heart health. See us promptly if an eyelid lump is hard, ulcerated, bleeding, or growing quickly, as that suggests something else.
