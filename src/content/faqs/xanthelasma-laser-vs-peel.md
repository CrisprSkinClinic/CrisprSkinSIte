---
question: "Is laser or a chemical peel better for xanthelasma?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

Neither is better for everyone. Both suit small, superficial patches. A peel is simple but needs careful technique to avoid scarring or pigment change, while laser or radiofrequency gives more control over depth. Deeper or larger patches often do better with excision. We recommend the method that fits your patches and skin type.
