---
question: "Can xanthelasma removal leave a scar?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

Any method can, because eyelid skin is thin and delicate. Excision leaves a fine scar that usually fades, while TCA touch and RFA can cause lighter or darker marks, more often in deeper skin tones. Larger excisions near the eyelid edge can pull the lid. Careful technique and choosing the right method for your patch lower these risks.
