---
question: "TCA touch, RFA, or excision: which is best for xanthelasma?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

None is best for everyone. A TCA touch suits small, flat patches and needs no cutting but is often repeated. RFA removes small to moderate patches layer by layer with good depth control and no stitches. Excision suits larger or deeper patches and tends to last longer but leaves a fine scar. We recommend the method that fits your patches and skin type.
