---
question: "What are the treatment options for xanthelasma?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

We offer three established methods: a TCA touch (spot application of trichloroacetic acid), surgical excision, and radiofrequency ablation (RFA). TCA touch suits small, flat patches, excision suits larger or deeper ones, and RFA suits small to moderate patches. The choice depends on size, depth, position, and skin type, and we also check your cholesterol.
