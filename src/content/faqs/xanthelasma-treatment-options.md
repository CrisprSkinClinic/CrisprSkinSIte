---
question: "What are the treatment options for xanthelasma?"
tags: ["xanthelasma"]
---

The main options are surgical excision, trichloroacetic acid peel, laser or radiofrequency ablation, and, for small patches, cryotherapy or electrosurgery. Excision is generally preferred for larger or deeper patches, while peels and ablative methods suit small, superficial ones. The choice depends on size, depth, position, and skin type, and we also check your cholesterol.
