Skin infections are among the commonest reasons for a visit to a dermatologist, and also among the most mismanaged, because several different problems look alike and because steroid creams, often bought without a prescription, make most of them worse.

## The Main Problems

- **[Scabies](/conditions/scabies):** a mite infestation causing intense itching, worse at night, and spread in families.
- **[Impetigo](/conditions/impetigo):** a bacterial infection with honey-coloured crusts, common in children.
- **[Folliculitis](/conditions/folliculitis) and boils:** infection of hair follicles, from small pimples to painful abscesses.

Ringworm and other fungal infections are covered on our [fungal infection page](/services/fungal-infections).

## Why a Correct Diagnosis Matters

Scabies is treated with a scabicide, impetigo with antibiotics, folliculitis according to whether it is bacterial, fungal or from shaving, and ringworm with antifungals. A steroid cream can hide scabies and make ringworm spread. Treating the wrong problem delays cure and fuels spread in the household.

## How We Treat

- **Scabies:** a cream applied from the neck down, or tablets, repeated after a week, together with everyone in close contact. Itch continues for weeks after the mites are gone. See [scabies treatment step by step](/blog/scabies-treatment-step-by-step-and-why-the-itch-lasts).
- **Impetigo:** crusts softened and removed, an antibiotic ointment, and tablets for widespread disease. See [impetigo in children](/blog/impetigo-in-children-treatment-school-and-spread).
- **Folliculitis and boils:** the cause is identified, and treatment is matched. See [folliculitis and boils](/blog/folliculitis-and-boils-causes-and-treatment).
- **Recurrent boils:** a search for causes and a plan to reduce carriage. See [recurrent boils and staph prevention](/blog/recurrent-boils-and-staph-prevention).

## Stopping Spread at Home

Scabies and impetigo spread through close contact, shared bedding and towels. Treat everyone at once, wash clothes and bedding hot, and keep affected children home as advised. See [scabies in families and schools](/blog/scabies-in-families-and-schools-preventing-reinfection).

## When to Seek Urgent Care

Fever, spreading redness, a swollen painful face, a boil on the face or spine, or a baby with a widespread rash needs prompt attention. People with diabetes or weak immunity should be seen early.

## What to Expect at Your Visit

We examine the skin, ask about contacts and previous treatment, take a swab or scraping if needed, give a written plan for you and your household, and arrange a review.
