Pattern hair loss is a gradual, progressive condition. Left alone, it keeps advancing. Two medicines have the strongest evidence for slowing it, and sometimes partly reversing it: **finasteride** (an oral tablet, for men) and **minoxidil** (a lotion or foam, and increasingly a low-dose tablet).

## How Well Do They Work?

In clinical trials, most men on finasteride for two years stopped losing hair, and roughly half or more regrew some hair. Minoxidil thickens hair in a good proportion of both men and women, and the combination tends to do better than either one alone. Hair that has already gone completely, in a smooth, shiny area, rarely comes back, which is why starting early matters. Read our full guide on [how effective medical treatment for hair loss really is](/blog/how-effective-is-medical-treatment-for-hair-loss).

## Oral Finasteride

Finasteride blocks the enzyme that turns testosterone into DHT, the hormone that shrinks scalp follicles. It is taken once a day and takes three to six months to show a benefit, with the best result by twelve months. See [oral finasteride: benefits and side effects](/blog/oral-finasteride-for-hair-loss-benefits-and-side-effects).

## Oral and Topical Minoxidil

Minoxidil does not act on hormones. It prolongs the growth phase of the hair cycle and increases the thickness of each hair. The low-dose oral tablet is convenient and often well tolerated, but it is prescribed off-label, and needs a blood pressure and pulse check and monitoring for side effects such as ankle swelling and extra body hair. See [what to know about oral minoxidil](/blog/oral-minoxidil-for-hair-loss-what-to-know).

## Stopping Means Losing the Gain

These medicines control the condition; they do not cure it. If you stop, the benefit fades over six to twelve months. Our guide on [what happens when you stop hair loss medicines](/blog/stopping-hair-loss-medicines-what-happens) explains how to plan around this.

## Medicines and Hair Transplant

A transplant moves hair from the back of the scalp, but does not stop the surrounding natural hair from thinning. Medical treatment protects what you have and is advised for most people before and after surgery. See [do you still need medicines after a hair transplant](/blog/medical-therapy-with-hair-transplant) and our [FUE hair transplant](/services/hair-transplant-fue) service.

## Women

Finasteride is not used in women who could become pregnant. Minoxidil, and in selected women anti-androgen medicines, are the main options. See [medical treatment for female pattern hair loss](/blog/medical-treatment-for-female-pattern-hair-loss) and our [female hair loss](/services/female-hair-loss) service.
