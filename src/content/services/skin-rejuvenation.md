

## What Skin Rejuvenation Means

Skin rejuvenation is a plan of treatments that improve the texture, tone and clarity of the skin, combined with the right daily skin care. It is not a single procedure. The aim is healthier, more even skin, not a different face.

## What We Can Address

- **Dullness and uneven tone,** from sun exposure and old marks.
- **Rough texture, large pores and shallow acne scars.**
- **Pigmentation and sun spots.**
- **Fine lines** and early signs of ageing.
- **Redness and visible vessels** in selected people.

## Building Blocks of a Plan

1. **Daily foundation:** broad-spectrum sunscreen, a gentle cleanser, a moisturiser and a night-time retinoid, which together do the most for long-term skin quality. See [retinol and retinoids: how to start](/blog/retinol-vs-retinoids-how-to-start).
2. **Chemical peels** for tone and texture. See [chemical peels](/services/chemical-peels).
3. **Microneedling** for texture and shallow scars. See [microneedling for acne scars](/blog/microneedling-for-acne-scars-what-to-expect).
4. **Laser and light treatments,** chosen with care for brown skin.
5. **Neuromodulators and fillers** for lines and volume, where appropriate. See [Botox](/services/botox) and [fillers](/services/fillers).

## How We Plan

We assess your skin type and tone, your main concerns and your tolerance for downtime, then build a stepwise plan, starting with the simplest measures. Treatments are spaced to let the skin recover, and goals are agreed before anything is done.

## What to Expect

Visible improvement usually builds over several weeks to months, with a series of sessions. Results fade without sun protection and maintenance. See [skin rejuvenation: what to expect](/blog/skin-rejuvenation-what-to-expect).

## Safety on Brown Skin

Indian skin marks easily after irritation, so we choose gentler options, test patches, and protect from sun strictly. More is not better.

## What We Do Not Do

We do not promise to make you look years younger, we do not use unproven injectable "boosters" or experimental treatments, and we advise against treatments you do not need.
