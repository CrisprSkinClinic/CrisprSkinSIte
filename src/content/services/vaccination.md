Vaccines are usually thought of as a childhood matter, but dermatology is where several of them matter most for adults. Shingles is a skin disease. HPV causes warts and, less often, cancers. And many of our patients are about to start medicines for psoriasis, eczema, hidradenitis or other conditions that lower the immune system, which changes which vaccines are safe and when to give them.

We offer **herpes zoster (shingles) vaccination**, **HPV vaccination**, and **vaccine planning before immunosuppressant treatment**. Vaccines that we do not give, such as routine childhood vaccines, are best taken from your paediatrician or physician, and we will tell you what is due.

## Shingles Vaccination

Shingles is a painful blistering rash that can leave nerve pain lasting months. The recombinant vaccine is not a live vaccine and is given as two doses. It is recommended for adults from 50 years, and for adults from 18 years who have, or are about to have, weakened immunity. Our guide on [who should take the shingles vaccine](/blog/shingles-vaccine-who-should-get-it) explains the details, and the page on [herpes zoster](/conditions/herpes-zoster) covers the illness itself.

## HPV Vaccination

The HPV vaccine prevents infection with the virus types behind genital warts and several cancers. It works best before exposure, but adults can still benefit. It does not treat warts you already have. Read [what the HPV vaccine does and does not do](/blog/hpv-vaccine-what-it-prevents-and-what-it-does-not), and see [HPV infection](/conditions/hpv-infection).

## Before Immunosuppressants and Biologics

Live vaccines are generally avoided once you are on strong immune-lowering treatment, and non-live vaccines respond better when given beforehand. Planning this a few weeks ahead avoids delaying your treatment. Our [pre-treatment vaccine checklist](/blog/vaccines-before-immunosuppressants-biologics-checklist) lists what we usually review, and our service for [biologics and advanced therapy](/services/biologics) explains the wider screening.

## What to Expect at Your Visit

We review your vaccination record, past illnesses, medicines and any allergies, explain the benefits and possible reactions in plain language, and agree a schedule. Please bring any vaccination certificates and a list of current medicines.

## A Measured Approach

Vaccination is advised only where it is indicated for you. We will tell you plainly when a vaccine is not needed, when it should wait, and when it is better given elsewhere.
