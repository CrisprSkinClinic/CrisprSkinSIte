When humidity falls, skin loses water faster than it can replace it. Cold air, hot showers, heaters and air-conditioning make this worse, and the result is tightness, flaking, itching and, in some people, cracks that bleed. This happens in Chennai too, where air-conditioned rooms and travel to colder places are often the trigger.

Most winter dryness is simple and treatable at home. A smaller group has an underlying condition, such as [eczema](/services/eczema), [psoriasis](/services/psoriasis) or contact dermatitis, that simply flares in the dry season. Treating it as "just dryness" with more cream delays the right treatment.

## Our Approach

We start with a [simple routine that works](/blog/winter-dry-skin-routine-that-works): a gentle cleanser, lukewarm water, and moisturiser applied within minutes of bathing. We then examine areas that tend to need more, including [heels and lips](/blog/cracked-heels-and-chapped-lips-in-winter), [hands](/conditions/hand-eczema) and the [scalp](/blog/winter-hair-fall-and-dry-scalp).

## For Children and Babies

Young skin loses water faster and flares easily. See our guide to [baby and child skin in winter](/blog/baby-and-child-skin-in-winter), and our [paediatric dermatology](/services/pediatric-dermatology) service for persistent problems.

## When to Book a Visit

Book if itching disturbs sleep, skin is cracking or oozing, a rash spreads, or a moisturiser routine has not helped within two weeks.

## What Happens at Your Visit

We ask about your skin, bathing habits, products, work and home environment, travel, medicines and any previous skin conditions. We examine the skin, scalp, hands and feet, and, when needed, take a scraping to rule out fungus, or advise tests for thyroid or iron problems. You leave with a plan in plain language, including which products to use, how often and how much, and when to return.

## Who Benefits Most From a Visit

- People with eczema, psoriasis or other long-term skin conditions.
- Older adults with itchy, dry skin.
- Parents of babies and children with recurring dryness or rashes.
- People with diabetes, thyroid disease or poor circulation.
- Anyone whose skin has not improved with a basic routine.
